Okuma Azores Blue 6000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Okuma Azores Blue 6000 (Z-6000H-BLUE) is a 6000-size spinning reel. It weighs 18.5 oz, brings in 41.2 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 29 lb. For surf or pier fishing, allow enough line for your cast with some left for a fish's run before deciding how much backing to use.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Okuma Azores Blue 6000

Best overall range: 30-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-20 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 30-50 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 12-20 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Okuma Azores Blue 6000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, inshore, and saltwater. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 30-50 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 12-20 lb mono range.

Okuma Azores Blue 6000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Azores Blue 6000?

30-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Azores Blue 6000 Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 310 yards, 16 lb / 215 yards, 20 lb / 180 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 270 yards, 65 lb / 180 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
