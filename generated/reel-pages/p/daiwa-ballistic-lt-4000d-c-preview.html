Daiwa Ballistic LT 4000D-C FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Ballistic LT 4000D-C?

15-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For inshore fishing, the lighter end helps preserve casting distance and line capacity. Move up when larger baits, current, or nearby structure call for more control, within your rod's line rating. Choose the leader for the fish and structure rather than simply matching the braid label.
