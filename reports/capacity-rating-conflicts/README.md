# Capacity-Rating Conflict Audit

268 of 1336 reel entries flagged; 1266 had both usable references. Sizes and retrieve variants count separately.

The check compares the estimates from mono and braid ratings at the same .010-inch diameter. It warns only when even the closest usable estimates differ by at least twofold. This is a cautious screening rule, not proof of incorrect manufacturer specifications. Unknown reference diameters and catalog assumptions can produce the gap. No calculations or ratings were changed.

## Verified Example

[Daiwa's official 2026 BG LT table](https://daiwa.us/collections/spin-reels/products/bg-copy) matches all three BG LT database entries. For BGLT3000D-XH, mono is 10 lb/280 yd and 12 lb/220 yd; J-Braid is 15 lb/250 yd and 20 lb/220 yd.

At .010 inch, the current mono anchors imply 403.2-431.2 yd; the braid anchors imply 160-178.2 yd. The closest estimates differ by 2.26x. The regular mono result remains 431.2 yd and the regular braid center remains 169.1 yd. Neither is a physically verified fill amount.
