SPRO Cast Control 8X

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Cast Control 8X is SPRO's multipurpose casting braid. Verified package-label diameters cover 10, 12, 16, 18, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 and 80 lb. The lighter fillers are 150 m / 164 yd and heavier fillers 300 m / 328 yd; the current web variants also include selected 3,000 m bulk sizes.

Construction
Japanese eight-strand braid, marketed by SPRO for multipurpose casting
Listed strengths
10-80 lb
Line type
Braid main line

Is Cast Control 8X right for your setup?

Cast Control spans lighter spinning-style work through heavier casting setups, but each strength has its own physical diameter. This is particularly important for the unusual 12, 16 and 18 lb steps. Choose the exact package rather than translating those labels to a familiar strength from another braid brand.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Cast Control 8X strength

The package image and current web SKU identify 16 lb at 0.18 mm. A 2026 catalog row instead says 15 lb. This pack uses the current 16 lb web/package identity and does not rename the existing 16 lb catalog record.

The verified 20 lb filler is 0.22 mm and 150 m; 30 lb is 0.24 mm and 300 m. Both diameter and package length change across that step.

The legacy 22 lb record has no matching current official variant. Newly offered 6 and 8 lb variants lack reviewed diameter labels, so they are not included in this numeric chart.

Specification-based guidance, not a ReelCalc hands-on performance test. Capacity results are estimates, not measured fills.

Backing, working line & leaders

Check the strength-to-length pairing

Do not assume the 300 m package is available for every lighter strength. Match the length to the exact SKU shown in the evidence.

Bulk is not a separate strength

A 3,000 m bulk package changes purchase quantity, not the selected pound label. Only the explicitly listed bulk strength combinations are included.

Keep the backing join out of casting reach

Select a working length with a reserve before filling unused volume with backing. The 164- or 328-yard label is a purchase limit, not a requirement to fill the reel with the entire package.

Cast Control 8X questions, answered

Is the middle size 15 or 16 lb?

The official 2026 catalog says 15 lb; current web variants and the actual package image say 16 lb. This guide uses 16 lb and flags the catalog disagreement rather than merging both.

Why is 22 lb not shown?

The legacy catalog includes it, but no matching current official product variant or label was verified.

Are the line-size numbers used to calculate diameter?

No. The package labels publish millimeters, which are used directly. The numbered size on the label is not converted into diameter.

How much Cast Control 8X will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 164-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
