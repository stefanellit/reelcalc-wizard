Fishing Reel Backing + Line Capacity Calculator

Use Simple Setup for a quick estimate when you have one line-capacity rating from your reel.

BACKING MODE Backing mode is selected: calculate backing + your chosen working line length.

Step 1: Reel Specs

Enter one complete Mono or Braid capacity rating from your reel.

Find the reel's line-capacity specifications on the spool, box, manual, or manufacturer's specifications.

Choose the unit beside each field to match the reel exactly. Mixed units are okay, such as 8 lb / 200 yards / 0.28 mm.

If the reel lists diameter instead, select "My reel lists diameter" and enter the printed diameter.

Step 2: Main Line

Enter the line you plan to put on the reel and how much of it you want to use.

Choose Braid for braided main line. Choose Mono / Fluoro for monofilament or fluorocarbon.

Enter the line's published diameter from its package or manufacturer specifications. Example: 0.006 in.

Enter how much main line you want over the backing. Example: 165 yards of 0.006 in braid.

Step 3: Backing

Select your backing line type and enter its diameter. ReelCalc will calculate how much backing you need underneath your chosen main-line amount.

Backing is the line underneath the main line. Choose Mono / Fluoro when the backing is mono or fluorocarbon, or Braid when it is braided line.

Enter the backing line's published diameter. You do not enter a backing amount because ReelCalc calculates it.

Example: 0.012 in mono backing.
