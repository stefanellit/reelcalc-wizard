Vicious Panfish Hi-Vis Yellow Mono

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Vicious Panfish mono in Hi-Vis Yellow comes in light 2, 4, 6 and 8 lb strengths. The range starts with 330-yard spools and includes larger spools in some strengths. This guide does not cover Panfish braid, clear mono or Ice mono.

Construction

A visible line for small presentations

Vicious targets this mono at panfish and other light-line presentations, with yellow chosen for above-water line visibility. Pick test for the fish, cover and small hook or lure rather than assuming the lowest number is always best. The current 2 lb diameter is .006 inch and 8 lb is .009 inch, so the size label alone is not a generic ultralight-diameter standard.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Panfish Hi-Vis Yellow strength

Match the 2-8 lb test to the rod and presentation. Thin light-test line needs an appropriate drag and care around cover, even when the reel can hold much more.

The manufacturer describes low memory, but this guide does not claim a hands-on small-jig test. Use the actual Panfish mono chart rather than Panfish braid dimensions.

The current 4 lb bulk options contain 2,960, 11,200 or 22,400 yards. Those are supply packages, not sensible single-reel targets.

This is a source-based specification and setup guide, not a hands-on review or comparative performance test.

Panfish Hi-Vis Yellow questions, answered

Does Hi-Vis Yellow mean invisible to fish?

No. It describes a visibility choice for the angler, not a promise of underwater invisibility.

Is the 2 lb diameter .005 inch?

The current official Panfish Mono chart lists .006 inch. The old catalog figure is not current source evidence.

Are 100-yard spools included?

No. The chart artwork shows them, but the checked current variants start at 330 yards. Only currently listed package combinations are used.

Can I substitute Panfish braid?

Not in this chart. Panfish braid is a separate line construction with its own diameters.
