Quantum Benchmark 6000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Quantum Benchmark 6000 (BK6000.B2) is a 6000-size spinning reel. It weighs 21.2 oz, brings in 41 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 35 lb. For surf or pier fishing, allow enough line for your cast with some left for a fish's run. Fill the spool with main line, or use backing under the amount you want to fish. The preloaded calculator below helps estimate either setup for this reel.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Quantum Benchmark 6000

Best overall range: 40-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 15-20 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 40-50 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 15-20 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Quantum Benchmark 6000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing sealed spinning, inshore, surf, saltwater, and heavy freshwater. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 40-50 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 15-20 lb mono range.

Quantum Benchmark 6000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Quantum Benchmark 6000?

40-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Quantum Benchmark 6000 Hold?

The reel is published at 20 lb / 315 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 375 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
