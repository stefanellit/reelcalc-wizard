Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 (QR750) is a 750-size spinning reel. It weighs 6.2 oz, brings in 22.8 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 7 lb. For small lures, start with line that suits your rod and check its diameter as well as its pound test. Use the line suggestions and preloaded calculator below to plan a full spool of main line or a shorter length over optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750

Best overall range: 4-8 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-4 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 4-8 lb braid and add a 2-4 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 2-4 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing ultralight trout, panfish, and creek fishing. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 2-4 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 4-8 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750

A practical starting point for the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 is 4 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 2-4 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 Specs

The Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 uses a 5.1:1 gear ratio and retrieves 22.8 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750?

4-8 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa QR Ultralight 750 Hold?

The reel is published at 4 lb / 110 yards, 6 lb / 80 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 8 lb / 100 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
