Shimano Kairiki 8 Braid

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Kairiki 8 is Shimano's eight-carrier VT Construction braid. This guide covers the Australian 3,000-meter bulk range, a supply for multiple reel fills rather than a single filler spool. The verified selection contains 15, 20, 30, 50 and 80 lb in Mantis Green and Multi.

Construction
Eight-carrier PE braid with VT Construction; Australian 3000m bulk SKU range
Listed strengths
15-80 lb
Line type
Braid main line

Is Kairiki 8 right for your setup?

Consider this bulk version when you maintain several reels or need repeat fills from one supply. A multicolor mainline can help track paid-out line, while Mantis Green provides a single-color choice. Carrier count and coating descriptions are not a substitute for matching strength, reserve and reel capacity to your fishing.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Kairiki 8 strength

The exact Australian 20 lb bulk diameter is .20 mm; 30 lb is .23 mm. These values belong to the SKE3000 SKU family, not an assumed universal Kairiki chart.

The 50 lb entry is .315 mm and 80 lb is .42 mm. The heavier line consumes appreciably more spool volume, so do not divide the bulk supply by an assumed equal fill length.

The official Australian filler rows lack diameters. Their 150- and 300-meter packages are documented separately but are not assigned bulk diameters here.

Specification-based guidance, not a hands-on Kairiki 8 test. Capacity results are estimates, not measured fills.

3,000 meters converts to approximately 3,280.84 yards. Choose a working length and reserve for each reel before deciding how many fills the supply will provide.

Keep an allowance for losses

Plan for future reties and damaged line removal, especially when dividing one bulk spool across several reels. A nominally exact division leaves no reserve for those losses.

Read the Australian code

The verified 20 lb codes are SKE300020G and SKE300020M. Shorter filler packages and other regions need their own diameter evidence; a similar name is not enough.

Kairiki 8 questions, answered

Does this cover the 150m filler spool?

No. The Australian page lists those packages but leaves their diameter cells blank. This guide uses only complete 3000m bulk rows.

Is Kairiki 8 the same as Kairiki 8+?

No. The plus model is a separate product identity. This page uses the standard Kairiki 8 bulk SKU range.

Can I divide a bulk spool equally among reels?

Only after checking each reel and desired working length. Different spool sizes and line diameters need different amounts, and future trimming requires reserve.
