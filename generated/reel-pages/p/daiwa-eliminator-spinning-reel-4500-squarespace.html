Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500 (ELT4500) falls in the 4500-size class and is intended for inshore, surf, salmon, and heavy freshwater. 21.7 oz, 43.1 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 17.6 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. The combination of 17.6 lb maximum drag and a 14 lb / 350 yard capacity baseline supports stronger line and fish that can make a sustained run.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500

Best overall range: 30-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-14 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 30-40 lb braid and add a 12-14 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-14 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500 Specs

Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500?

30-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 4500 Hold?

The reel is published at 14 lb / 350 yards, 17 lb / 280 yards, 20 lb / 210 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 40 lb / 340 yards, 50 lb / 270 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
