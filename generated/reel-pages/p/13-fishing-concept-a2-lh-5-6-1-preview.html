13 Fishing Concept A2 LH 5.6:1 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the 13 Fishing Concept A2 LH 5.6:1?

30-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this baitcaster. For casting lures, choose within that range to suit your rod and presentation. The lighter end suits open water; move up when your rig and fishing conditions call for more strength. Do not choose the thinnest braid just to gain yardage: braid can dig into lower wraps on a baitcaster under load.

How Much Line Does the 13 Fishing Concept A2 LH 5.6:1 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 12 lb / 125 yards mono. The cited source does not list a braid capacity for this exact model. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
