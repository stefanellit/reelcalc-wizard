Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano Sedona FI 1000

Best overall range: 4-8 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

4-8 lb braid is a ReelCalc starting suggestion for this setup. For a straight main line, consider 2-6 lb mono/fluoro. Match the strength to your rod, presentation and cover; backing is optional.

Shimano Sedona FI 1000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Sedona FI 1000?

4-8 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Choose within that range to suit your rod, lure or bait, and the cover you fish. The lighter end helps with smaller presentations and line capacity; the stronger end offers more margin when the rig or cover calls for it. Choose a leader to suit the fish and conditions.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Sedona FI 1000 Hold?
