Abu Garcia Ambassadeur C2 6600 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Abu Garcia Ambassadeur C2 6600?

Start with 30-50 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. Very thin braid can dig into lower wraps on a baitcaster, so maximum yardage is not the only consideration. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the Abu Garcia Ambassadeur C2 6600 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
