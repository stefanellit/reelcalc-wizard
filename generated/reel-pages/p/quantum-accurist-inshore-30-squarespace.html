Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 (ATS30SPT) is a 30-size spinning reel suited to inshore, saltwater, bass, and walleye. 9.1 oz, 31 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 18 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. At 9.1 oz, this size balances repeated casting with the line reserve expected for light saltwater and inshore work.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-10 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-20 lb braid and add an 8-10 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 8-10 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, saltwater, bass, and walleye. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 8-10 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-20 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30

A practical starting point for the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 is 10 lb braid over monofilament backing with an 8-10 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Quantum Accurist Inshore 30 Hold?

The reel is published at 10 lb / 150 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 220 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
