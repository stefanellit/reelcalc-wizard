Shimano Sedona FI 6000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Sedona FI 6000?

30-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For pier fishing, use the lighter end when casting distance and line capacity matter most. Move toward the stronger end around structure, heavier current, or larger baits, while staying within your rod's line rating. Match the leader to the structure and fish; heavier braid alone is not a substitute for a suitable leader.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Sedona FI 6000 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 16 lb / 170 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
