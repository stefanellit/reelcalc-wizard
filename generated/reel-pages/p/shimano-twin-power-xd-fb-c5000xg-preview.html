Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG (TPXDC5000XGFB) is a 5000-size spinning reel. MGL Rotor uses lightweight CI4+ construction for easy start-and-stop retrieves, while Anti-Twist Fin helps keep slack line from dropping below the spool. For fishing in current, allow enough line for your cast or drift with some left for a fish's run. The calculator below is set up for this exact reel: estimate a full spool of your chosen main line, or add backing underneath a shorter amount.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG

Best overall range: 30-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-14 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 30-40 lb braid and add a 12-14 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-14 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, surf, salmon, and heavy freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 12-14 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 30-40 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG Specs

The Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG uses a 6.2:1 gear ratio and retrieves 40 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 8.6 oz and has a published maximum drag of 24 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG?

30-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Twin Power XD FB C5000XG Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 195 yards, 14 lb / 165 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 260 yards, 30 lb / 235 yards, 40 lb / 185 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
