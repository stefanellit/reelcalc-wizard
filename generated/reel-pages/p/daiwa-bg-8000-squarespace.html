Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa BG 8000

Best overall range: 50-65 lb braid

Simple alternative: 16-20 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 50-65 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 16-20 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa BG 8000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, pier, offshore, and heavy bait fishing. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 50-65 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 16-20 lb mono range.

Daiwa BG 8000 Specs

The Daiwa BG 8000 uses a 5.3:1 gear ratio and retrieves 53.3 inches per handle turn. It weighs 30 oz and has a published maximum drag of 33 lb, reflecting its focus on capacity and pulling power rather than light-tackle handling. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Daiwa BG 8000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa BG 8000?

50-65 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa BG 8000 Hold?

The reel is published at 20 lb / 550 yards, 25 lb / 440 yards, 30 lb / 370 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 65 lb / 590 yards, 80 lb / 440 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
