Rhino Hide Camo Monofilament

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Rhino Hide Camo Monofilament is Zebco's Green Camo mono in 330-yard filler spools, with checked options from 6 to 30 lb. It is distinct from the 200-yard Rhino Hide braid.

A camo mono working line

Choose Rhino Hide mono when a single-strand mainline suits the presentation and you want the Green Camo offering. Test and diameter need to suit the reel as well as the fishing: the 30 lb option is more than twice the listed inch diameter of the 6 lb option. A line fitting by volume does not establish comfortable handling on a small spinning spool.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Rhino Hide Mono strength

Start with the lighter 6-10 lb options only where the rod, lure and target fish justify them. Check spool handling before treating the capacity result as a recommendation.

The 12-20 lb group occupies more volume and is a different choice from fine mono. Choose it for tackle and cover requirements rather than copying a reel's printed capacity test.

At 25-30 lb, the physical diameter matters considerably. Keep these rows separate from Rhino braid of the same test.

This is a source-based specification and setup guide, not a hands-on review or comparative performance test.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Check the RNMF330 package

Confirm mono, strength and the 330-yard label before planning the fill. The similarly named RNB200 braid is not interchangeable.

Choose full mono or a working section

A full fill avoids a backing junction. A shorter mono working section can use backing below it when the reel holds more line than needed; keep the connection out of normal casts.

Stop at the reel's fill limit

Use steady tension and the recommended spool-lip clearance. Keep unused line for another fill instead of overfilling to finish the package.

Rhino Hide Mono questions, answered

Is Rhino Hide mono the same as the braid?

No. This is RNMF330 monofilament in 330-yard spools. RNB200 is braid in 200-yard spools.

Are all millimeter figures manufacturer measurements?

No. The checked attributes publish mm for 8 and 25 lb, and the official 10 lb package prints .286 mm. Other mm values are converted from published inches.

Will 330 yards fill two reels?

Only when their combined planned working fills fit within 330 yards, allowing for waste and knots. Backing can fill unused spool volume; it does not add working-line length.

Does a capacity fit prove 30 lb is suitable?

No. Rod rating, lure, cover and handling on the reel still determine suitability.
