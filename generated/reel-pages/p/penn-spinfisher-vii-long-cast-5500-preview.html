PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500 (SSVII5500LC) is a 5500-size spinning reel suited to inshore, surf, pier, and live bait. 20.7 oz, 36 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 20 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. Its published 10 lb / 310 yard capacity baseline leaves room for working line and leader, while the 36-inch pickup supports quick line control around moving fish.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500

Best overall range: 20-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-15 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-40 lb braid and add a 12-15 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-15 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500?

20-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the PENN Spinfisher VII Long Cast 5500 Hold?

The reel is published at 8 lb / 355 yards, 10 lb / 310 yards, 12 lb / 215 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 450 yards, 20 lb / 380 yards, 30 lb / 265 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
