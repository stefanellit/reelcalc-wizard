Sunline Kantan Cast Braid X8

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Kantan Cast Braid X8 is Sunline's braid for repeated casting and power-fishing presentations such as pitching, flipping and frogs. This guide covers the verified 30 and 50 lb US rows, each in 150- and 600-yard packages.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Kantan Cast Braid X8 strength

30 lb is listed at .296 mm; 50 lb is .382 mm. Select the actual row before estimating the fill, especially with a shallow spool.

The 30 lb spool pictured identifies the family. It does not mean the 50 lb row has the same diameter or that every strength shares one label.

A short leader does not change the diameter of the braid occupying most of the spool. Calculate with the main line, then choose the leader for the presentation.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Choose the working section first

Allow for the longest cast, fish runs and later trimming. Compare that total with the chosen package; a 150-yard supply need not fill the reel to its full braid capacity.

Use backing deliberately

Follow the reel maker's arbor-attachment guidance. When using mono backing, leave space for the intended braid section rather than winding an arbitrary amount underneath it.

Treat bulk length as a budget

A 600-yard spool can be divided among planned fills. Four 150-yard portions are not necessarily four suitable reel fills; account for each reel and attachment allowance.

Kantan Cast Braid X8 questions, answered

Is Kantan Cast Braid X8 fluorocarbon?

No. It is braid. Sunline's handling comparison does not change its material identity.

Why are only 30 and 50 lb shown?

These are the coherent source-verified rows used here. The 40 and 60 lb manufacturer inch/mm entries still need clarification. No 65 lb variant was established in the checked US listing.

Does the photograph show every package?

No. It shows the original 30 lb, 150-yard spool. The manufacturer separately lists both included strengths in 150- and 600-yard packages.

Specifications & sources

The Sunline America product chart and listed spool sizes provide the numeric evidence. The dealer-hosted original spool label supplies exact-family imagery. No retailer-authored diameter correction is used.
