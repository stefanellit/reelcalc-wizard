Best overall range: 30-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-15 lb mono/fluoro

Who Is the Lew's MACH Bottomland Baitcast 150 For?

For bass fishing with moving baits, start with the rod's line range and the cover you expect to fish. ReelCalc's 30-50 lb braid and 10-15 lb mono/fluoro ranges are setup suggestions, not extra manufacturer ratings. Choose the lighter end for open water, and move up when the rod, cover and presentation call for it.

Lew's MACH Bottomland Baitcast 150 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's MACH Bottomland Baitcast 150?

Start with 30-50 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. Very thin braid can dig into lower wraps on a baitcaster, so maximum yardage is not the only consideration. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the Lew's MACH Bottomland Baitcast 150 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
