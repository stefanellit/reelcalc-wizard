PENN Fierce III 2000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Fierce III 2000?

8-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For inshore fishing, the lighter end helps preserve casting distance and line capacity. Move up when larger baits, current, or nearby structure call for more control, within your rod's line rating. Choose the leader for the fish and structure rather than simply matching the braid label.

How Much Line Does the PENN Fierce III 2000 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 6 lb / 180 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
