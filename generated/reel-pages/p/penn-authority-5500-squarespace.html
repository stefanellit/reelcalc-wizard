PENN Authority 5500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The PENN Authority 5500 (ATH5500) is a 5500-size spinning reel suited to inshore, surf, pier, and live bait. 23.6 oz, 36 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 40 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. At 23.6 oz, this size balances repeated casting with the line reserve expected for light saltwater and inshore work.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Authority 5500

Best overall range: 20-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-15 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-40 lb braid and add a 12-15 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-15 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Authority 5500 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, surf, pier, and live bait. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 12-15 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 20-40 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the PENN Authority 5500

A practical starting point for the PENN Authority 5500 is 20 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 12-15 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

PENN Authority 5500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Authority 5500?

20-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the PENN Authority 5500 Hold?

The reel is published at 10 lb / 430 yards, 12 lb / 330 yards, 15 lb / 230 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 500 yards, 30 lb / 380 yards, 40 lb / 335 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
