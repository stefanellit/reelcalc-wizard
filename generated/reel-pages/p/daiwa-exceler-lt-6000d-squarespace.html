Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D (EXELT6000D) is a 6000-size spinning reel. It weighs 10.8 oz, brings in 36.2 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 26.4 lb. For surf or pier fishing, allow enough line for your cast with some left for a fish's run. Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D

Best overall range: 40-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 14-20 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 40-50 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 14-20 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, inshore, and larger saltwater. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 40-50 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 14-20 lb mono range.

Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D Specs

Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D?

40-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Exceler LT 6000D Hold?

The reel is published at 20 lb / 270 yards, 30 lb / 160 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 30 lb / 320 yards, 40 lb / 240 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
