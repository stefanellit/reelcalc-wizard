Quick Answer: Best Line for the Okuma Alaris 20

Best overall range: 4-8 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

4-8 lb braid is a ReelCalc starting suggestion for this setup. For a straight main line, consider 2-6 lb mono/fluoro. Match the strength to your rod, presentation and cover; backing is optional.

Okuma Alaris 20 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Alaris 20?

4-8 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when fishing small lures for trout and panfish in open water. The upper end can provide more margin around light cover, but keep the line matched to the rod and lure so the setup still handles light presentations well.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Alaris 20 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 4 lb / 140 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
