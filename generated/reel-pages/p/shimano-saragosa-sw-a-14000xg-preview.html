Who Is the Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf casting, offshore, and big saltwater. Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG Specs

The Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG uses a 6.2:1 gear ratio and retrieves 53 inches per handle turn. It weighs 24.7 oz and has a published maximum drag of 33 lb, reflecting its focus on capacity and pulling power rather than light-tackle handling. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG?

65-80 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Saragosa SW A 14000XG Hold?

The reel is published at 16 lb / 360 yards, 20 lb / 260 yards, 25 lb / 190 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 400 yards, 65 lb / 315 yards, 80 lb / 240 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
