Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C (EXELT5000D-C) is a 5000-size spinning reel. It weighs 8.1 oz, brings in 34.5 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 26.4 lb. For fishing in current, allow enough line for your cast or drift with some left for a fish's run.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C

Best overall range: 30-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-14 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 30-40 lb braid and add a 12-14 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-14 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, surf, salmon, and heavy freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 12-14 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 30-40 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C

A practical starting point for the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C is 30 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 12-14 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C Specs

Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C?

30-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Exceler LT 5000D-C Hold?

The reel is published at 14 lb / 280 yards, 20 lb / 180 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 30 lb / 230 yards, 40 lb / 160 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
