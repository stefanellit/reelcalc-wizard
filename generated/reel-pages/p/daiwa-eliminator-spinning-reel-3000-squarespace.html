The Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 (ELT3000) is a 3000-size spinning reel. It weighs 11.3 oz, brings in 37.4 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 15.4 lb. Match your main line and leader to the fish and structure you expect to encounter. Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-10 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-20 lb braid and add an 8-10 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 8-10 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing bass, walleye, light inshore, and all-around spinning. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 8-10 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-20 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000

A practical starting point for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 is 10 lb braid over monofilament backing with an 8-10 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 Specs

The Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 uses a 5.6:1 gear ratio and retrieves 37.4 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 11.3 oz and has a published maximum drag of 15.4 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 3000 Hold?

The reel is published at 8 lb / 240 yards, 10 lb / 200 yards, 12 lb / 170 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 240 yards, 30 lb / 190 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
