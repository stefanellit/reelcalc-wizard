A bulk-mainline reference, not a full strength range

Use this reference when the labeled 2021 US Tactical 15 lb specification is the one you need to evaluate. Its 1,000-yard package describes supply for multiple potential fills, not a recommendation to put that much fluorocarbon on one reel. Suitability still depends on the rod, lure, cover, and required working length. A historical specification cannot establish the condition of a particular stored spool.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Separate bulk supply from a reel fill

Budget enough working Tactical for your fishing, then compare it with the reel's diameter-based capacity. A 1,000-yard package can be divided into planned fills; it is not a reel-size recommendation.

Reserve working line above backing

Backing can occupy the lower spool while Tactical remains the working main line. Keep the join below the line expected to leave the reel, allowing for runs and retying.

Do not turn a mainline label into a leader rating

The source is a Tactical mainline bulk spool, not a leader-specific product chart. Short leader use and long topshots need their own material, length, and knot decisions.

Tactical questions, answered

Why does this page say 2021 US reference?

The exact readable 15 lb diameter and 1,000-yard package are documented in the official 2021 US catalog. The page deliberately makes no claim that these are the current edition or available stock.

Why is 17 lb omitted?

The photographed 200-yard label prints .0154 inch and .393 mm. Their difference is .00184 mm, just beyond the combined .00177 mm half-unit rounding allowance. Both values are preserved in the research, but the conflicting row is not used.

Can I select a 200-yard spool for the 15 lb row?

Not from this reference. The verified 15 lb package is PTB1000-15 at 1,000 yards; a family-level filler option does not independently establish the same numeric specification.

Does this establish that an older Tactical spool is safe to fish?

No. The catalog establishes a historical label specification, not storage history or present condition. Inspect the actual line and follow appropriate tackle and reel guidance.

Specifications & sources

Numeric authority is the manufacturer-printed PTB1000-15 bulk-spool label in P-Line's official 2021 US catalog, page 6. The historical year is supported by the official filename and PDF metadata, not inferred from today's listing. Current official variants corroborate the identity only. The 17 lb label remains a documented unit conflict; neither printed value was corrected or preferred.

Calculations use the catalog's listed inch diameters. Published inch and millimeter values may differ slightly because of rounding.
