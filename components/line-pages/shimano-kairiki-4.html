Is Kairiki 4 right for your setup?

Consider Kairiki 4 when a four-carrier mainline and its available diameters fit your tackle. The 150-, 300-, and 3,000-meter packages are not equivalent yard labels. Compare the physical diameter and usable reserve before deciding which spool to divide among reels.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Kairiki 4 strength

The official table pairs 10 lb with .13 mm, 15 lb with .16 mm, and 20 lb with .20 mm. The starting catalog's values should be replaced only with these exact verified rows.

The intermediate 18 and 25 lb entries are actual table labels. Do not round them to 20 or 30 lb or relabel the corresponding existing IDs.

The 50 lb .315 mm row is verified only as a 150-meter Multi Color SKU in this source. Bulk lengths from lighter strengths cannot be assigned to it.

Backing, working line & leaders

Keep metric packages intact

150 meters is about 164.04 yards and 300 meters is about 328.08 yards. Retail links should retain the meter package identity rather than advertise a fabricated 150-yard spool.

Choose bulk deliberately

A 3,000-meter package is a multi-reel supply where verified. Divide it by required working lengths and reserve, not by the pound labels alone.

Separate title strength from table labels

This regional table labels its pound column as average strength, while some product titles give different kilogram figures. The page reports the exact table labels and makes no independent breaking-strength guarantee.

Kairiki 4 questions, answered

Is Kairiki 4 the same as Kairiki 8?

No. The carrier count and product identity differ. This guide uses Kairiki 4 SKU rows only.

Does 300m mean 300yd?

No. It is about 328.08 yards. The converted value is a calculation aid, not a second package option.

Why are 18 and 25 lb included?

They are explicit pound values in Shimano's European specification table. They have not been rounded to more familiar labels.
