Seaguar JDM PEX8 Braid

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

JDM PEX8 is Seaguar's eight-strand metered braid for finesse-oriented applications. It pairs a pink main color with contrasting green markings and is offered on 200-meter spools, labeled approximately 219 yards. Its six strengths run from 12 to 33 lb.

Construction
8-strand metered braid
Listed strengths
12-33 lb
Line type
Braid main line

Is JDM PEX8 right for your setup?

PEX8 is a candidate for anglers who want a visible, thin braid for presentations such as drop-shot fishing. Its strength labels should not be treated as direct handling equivalents to thicker conventional braids. Check diameter, knot management, and the reel as well as the breaking-strength number; 33 lb on this package does not automatically make it a heavy-cover braid setup.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your JDM PEX8 strength

Retain the exact 12, 16, 18, 21, 24, or 33 lb choice. These are manufacturer-listed strengths, not rounded substitutes for 10, 15, 20, or 30 lb. The published diameter is the quantity used in the capacity calculation.

The 12-18 lb entries occupy very little spool space at .005-.006 inch. A high calculated capacity can make backing useful, but do not confuse that capacity with advice to use these fine diameters on every baitcaster.

Even the 33 lb entry is listed at .009 inch. Choose a leader and drag setting for the presentation rather than assuming the large pound-test label permits heavy loading. Thin braid still needs careful knots and even winding tension.

This is a specification-based setup guide, not a hands-on JDM PEX8 performance test.

Backing, working line & leaders

Budget the 200-meter spool

The retail length is 200 meters, approximately 219 yards. Compare that with your intended working section or full-spool result, not with a guessed 200-yard package.

Use backing for unused volume

A thin braid may leave substantial unused space when you install only the working length you need. Mono backing can occupy that space and provide an arbor anchor; choose its exact diameter rather than copying a generic backing yardage.

Metering is not a line counter

Colored sections can help track line in use, but slack, angle, and current can separate line-out from actual lure depth. Do not present the color pattern as a precise depth measurement or a substitute for measuring backing.

JDM PEX8 questions, answered

Is PEX8 a PE size number?

PEX8 is the product name, and the 8 refers to its eight-strand construction in this guide. Select the listed pound-test variant and numeric diameter; do not enter 8 as a PE rating merely because it appears in the name.

Why do 16 lb and 18 lb show the same capacity?

Both round to .006 inch in the source, though their published millimeter diameters differ. ReelCalc consistently uses the inch entries, so equal results reflect rounded source precision rather than proving equal physical diameters.

Does 33 lb PEX8 replace any other 30 lb braid?

Not automatically. Compare actual diameter and the demands of the setup. A thinner braid may fit more line but handle differently on the spool, particularly under load on a baitcaster.
