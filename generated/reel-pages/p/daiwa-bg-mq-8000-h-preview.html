Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H (BGMQ8000-H) falls in the 8000-size class and is best matched to surf, pier, offshore, and heavy bait fishing. 22.4 oz, 43.4 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 33.1 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. At 22.4 oz, this is deliberately a power-and-capacity reel rather than a light-tackle all-rounder.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H

Best overall range: 50-65 lb braid

Simple alternative: 16-20 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 50-65 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 16-20 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H?

50-65 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa BG MQ 8000-H Hold?

The reel is published at 16 lb / 330 yards, 20 lb / 280 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 40 lb / 330 yards, 50 lb / 280 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
