Choosing and using Quattro Fluorocarbon

Choose leader test and diameter for the rod, drag, hook, lure and cover, not by copying the braid label.

The included 8, 10 and 25 lb sizes are .30, .32 and .45 mm respectively. The 25 lb package photo corroborates its title; 8 and 10 lb are title-sourced. The omitted 12, 15, 20 and 30 lb options need manufacturer clarification of conflicting diameter evidence.

A 25-yard package supplies multiple terminal sections. Allow for knots, tag ends, trimming and replacements, and inspect the leader after contact with structure.

Official-specification leader guidance; no hands-on test, underwater-invisibility guarantee or full-spool recommendation.

Leader stock, not a reel fill

Each verified CFQ-B25 SKU supplies 25 yards for making terminal sections. It is not a full-spool capacity recommendation.

Keep the role distinction

Backing sits below the mainline; this Quattro leader belongs at the terminal end. A long topshot requires a separate spool-volume plan.

Plan the line on your reel separately

A short terminal leader is not the working line that fills your spool. Use the actual main line in the calculator or Setup Wizard. A long topshot that occupies substantial spool space needs its own capacity plan.

Quattro Fluorocarbon questions, answered

Why are several strengths missing?

Current official SKU titles and exact package photos disagree on diameter at 12, 15, 20 and 30 lb. Those options are excluded rather than choosing a conflicting figure by assumption.

Is Quattro mono the same material?

No. Hi-Seas sells Quattro Monofilament separately. These CFQ-B25 SKUs explicitly identify 100% fluorocarbon leader.

Does 25 yards specify leader length per rig?

No. It is the supplied length. Choose each terminal section for the fishing and allow for knots, tags and trimming.

Are all included diameters backed by readable package photos?

The 25 lb title and image agree. The 8 lb image is generic and the 10 lb image is blank, so those two rows rely on their current official SKU titles.
