P-Line CX Premium

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

CX Premium combines copolymer with a fluorocarbon-silicone coating. This US reference covers the photographed Moss Green CXFMG 6 and 8 lb, 300-yard cartons. Their production years are unestablished; the labels do not establish a current edition or stock position. CX Premium is not CXX X-Tra Strong.

A coated main line for a measured spool plan

CX Premium is a coated-copolymer mainline option, with P-Line discussing both spinning and baitcasting use. The supported 6 and 8 lb packages let you compare .0084 and .0092 inch diameters without borrowing the specifications of CXX or Clear Fluorescent CX. Choose an appropriate rod and lure combination first; a larger capacity estimate is not a reason to use a lighter line than the fishing needs.

Both accepted cartons contain 300 yards. No bulk or other-color package is silently added to either row.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Check the exact CX carton

The accepted packages say CX Premium, CXFMG, Moss Green, and 300 yards. CXX and other CX colors are separate source identities even when their packaging looks similar.

Use backing only below the working line

For a shorter working fill, allow enough CX for the cast, reserve, and retying, then use backing for the remaining spool volume. Keep the join below the expected working section.

Stop at the physical fill level

Wind under even tension and follow the reel maker's fill guidance. The .0084- and .0092-inch labels support calculated estimates, not a promise that every reel will accept the exact predicted yardage.

CX Premium questions, answered

Is CX Premium the same as CXX?

No. CX Premium is the coated copolymer identified by the CXFMG cartons here. CXX X-Tra Strong is a separate P-Line product and must use its own specification.

Which color and package are covered?

Only US Moss Green CXFMG 6 and 8 lb in 300-yard cartons. Clear Fluorescent, other strengths, and regional lengths are not established by these photos.

Are these verified current-production specifications?

They are verified values on readable production labels, with matching official US product identities. The photos do not establish production year, latest formulation, or present availability.

Is CX Premium a 100% fluorocarbon line?

No. P-Line describes a copolymer line with a fluorocarbon-silicone coating. This page preserves its Copolymer mainline classification.

Specifications & sources

Actual eBay-hosted CX Premium carton photographs supply the numeric values. Official P-Line US model numbers CXFMG-6 and CXFMG-8 independently match the color, strength, and 300-yard package. The current generic 300YD/247M option contains a metric discrepancy and is not used to infer a meter-rated package. Retailer-authored diameters are excluded.

Calculations use the catalog's listed inch diameters. Published inch and millimeter values may differ slightly because of rounding.
