Pflueger Patriarch 35 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Pflueger Patriarch 35 (PARSP35X PFL) falls in the 35-size class and is intended for premium bass, river smallmouth, and light pike. 7.6 oz, 35 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 12 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. At 7.6 oz with 12 lb of maximum drag, it favors line control and pulling margin over ultralight handling.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Pflueger Patriarch 35

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-10 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-20 lb braid and add an 8-10 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 8-10 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Pflueger Patriarch 35 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing premium bass, river smallmouth, and light pike. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 8-10 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-20 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Pflueger Patriarch 35

A practical starting point for the Pflueger Patriarch 35 is 10 lb braid over monofilament backing with an 8-10 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Pflueger Patriarch 35 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Pflueger Patriarch 35?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Pflueger Patriarch 35 Hold?

The reel is published at 6 lb / 180 yards, 8 lb / 140 yards, 10 lb / 110 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 8 lb / 200 yards, 10 lb / 150 yards, 14 lb / 110 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
