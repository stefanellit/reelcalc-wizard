Yo-Zuri SuperBraid 9

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

SuperBraid 9 adds a straight PE core to eight surrounding strands. The reviewed solid-color lineup covers 8-100 lb with strength-specific 150-, 300- and 3,000-yard options. White and Dark Green do not have identical package ranges.

Construction
Eight PE strands braided around one straight PE core strand (8+1)
Listed strengths
8-100 lb
Line type
Braid main line

Is SuperBraid 9 right for your setup?

SuperBraid 9 is a candidate for a braid working fill when you want this specific core construction. Thin sizes can leave considerable unused spool volume on a larger reel, while the heavier range calls for suitable tackle and more working reserve. Do not assume the original SuperBraid package list applies unchanged to the 9-strand product.

Choosing your SuperBraid 9 strength

The 8 and 10 lb rows are 0.13 and 0.15 mm. For lighter tackle, consider the rod and leader connection before selecting the smallest diameter simply to maximize capacity.

The 150-yard range stops at 50 lb in Dark Green and at 20 lb in White. The 65-100 lb sizes require a different verified package selection.

The 80 and 100 lb solid-color rows are verified in White at 300 yards. One White 100 lb bulk description incorrectly says 65 lb; that ambiguous 3,000-yard package is withheld.

SuperBraid 9 questions, answered

What does the ninth strand do?

Yo-Zuri describes eight PE strands around a straight PE core and attributes durability and handling benefits to that structure. ReelCalc has not independently tested those performance claims.

Are White and Dark Green offered in identical sizes?

No. The official tables show different upper strengths and filler combinations. Use the exact package rows.

Why is the 100 lb bulk option absent?

The White bulk row's item code and strength column say 100 lb, but its description says 65 lb. The unambiguous 100 lb/300 yd row is retained; the conflicting bulk package is not.

How much SuperBraid 9 will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 150-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
