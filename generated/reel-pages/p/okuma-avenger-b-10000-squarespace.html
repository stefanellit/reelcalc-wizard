Who Is the Okuma Avenger B 10000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, offshore, and heavy saltwater. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 50-80 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 20-30 lb mono range.

Okuma Avenger B 10000 Specs

The Okuma Avenger B 10000 uses a 4.4:1 gear ratio and retrieves 37 inches per handle turn. Okuma Avenger B 10000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Avenger B 10000?

50-80 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Avenger B 10000 Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 550 yards, 16 lb / 375 yards, 20 lb / 305 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
