Okuma Cedros 14000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Okuma Cedros 14000 (CJ-14000) falls in the 14000-size class and is best matched to surf, offshore, and heavy saltwater. 22.8 oz, 45.8 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 44 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. The 45.8-inch pickup moves substantial line per crank, while 44 lb of maximum drag supports heavier braid setups.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Okuma Cedros 14000

Best overall range: 50-80 lb braid

Simple alternative: 20-30 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 50-80 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 20-30 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Okuma Cedros 14000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Cedros 14000?

50-80 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Cedros 14000 Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 690 yards, 16 lb / 470 yards, 20 lb / 380 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 65 lb / 410 yards, 85 lb / 370 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
