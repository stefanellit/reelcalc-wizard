Choosing and using Fluorocarbon Leader

Choose leader test for drag setting, hook and lure size, cover and target species; do not copy the mainline label automatically.

Within the checked clear range, 10-15 lb is supplied in 50-yard wrist skeins; 20-30 lb also has 25-yard variants. Package length is inventory for multiple leaders, not leader length per rig.

Inspect the section nearest the lure after contact with structure and replace damaged line. A thicker leader is not proof against teeth.

Specification-based leader guidance, not a hands-on test. No full-spool mainline recommendation.

A terminal section, not backing

Attach the leader at the lure end. Mono backing belongs underneath the working mainline; this product is not a backing calculator input by default.

Count leaders from usable package length

Divide package length by your chosen leader length only for a rough upper bound. Knots, tags, trimming and damaged sections reduce the number of finished leaders.

Plan the line on your reel separately

A short terminal leader is not the working line that fills your spool. Use the actual main line in the calculator or Setup Wizard. A long topshot that occupies substantial spool space needs its own capacity plan.

Fluorocarbon Leader questions, answered

Is this Ande Fluorocarbon 2.0?

No. ANDE sells 2.0 separately. These diameters and packages come from the original Fluorocarbon Leader product.

Does 50 yards mean I should use a 50-yard leader?

No. It is the amount supplied. Choose the rig's leader length separately; a long topshot also needs a reel-capacity plan.

Are the pink sizes included?

No. These rows deliberately use the clear variants so a different color offering is not assumed to share every package option.
