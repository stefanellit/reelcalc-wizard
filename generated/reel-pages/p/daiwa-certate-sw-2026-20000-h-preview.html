Daiwa Certate SW 2026 20000-H FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Certate SW 2026 20000-H?

80-100 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For offshore fishing, balance the working line and reserve you need against the strength required by your rod, rig, and target fish. The lighter end leaves more line in reserve; the stronger end suits heavier rigs when the rest of the tackle is matched to them. Choose the leader separately for the fish and structure.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Certate SW 2026 20000-H Hold?

Daiwa lists J-Braid at 80 lb / 400 yards and 100 lb / 330 yards. No mono capacity is published in the cited 2026 U.S. table. The calculator can estimate other lines from the available reference, but that is not a factory mono rating.
