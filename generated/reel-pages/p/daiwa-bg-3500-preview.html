Daiwa BG 3500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa BG 3500 (BG3500) is a 3500-size spinning reel suited to larger bass, pike, catfish, and light saltwater. ALUMINIUM BODY uses a rigid aluminum housing to limit flex under load, while DIGIGEAR uses digitally designed gearing for smoother power transfer. Its published 10 lb / 240 yard capacity baseline leaves room for working line and leader, while the 38.5-inch pickup supports quick line control around moving fish.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa BG 3500

Best overall range: 20-30 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-12 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-30 lb braid and add a 10-12 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 10-12 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa BG 3500 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing larger bass, pike, catfish, and light saltwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 10-12 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 20-30 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa BG 3500

A practical starting point for the Daiwa BG 3500 is 20 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 10-12 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Daiwa BG 3500 Specs

The Daiwa BG 3500 uses a 5.7:1 gear ratio and retrieves 38.5 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 14.1 oz and has a published maximum drag of 17.6 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Daiwa BG 3500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa BG 3500?

20-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa BG 3500 Hold?

The reel is published at 10 lb / 240 yards, 12 lb / 210 yards, 14 lb / 170 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 310 yards, 30 lb / 230 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
