Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Hi-Seas Grand Slam Braid, scoped to Green GSB-F300 300-yard SKUs. Official product titles verify 10, 15, 20, 30, 50 and 65 lb. This is the mainline braid, not the separate Downrigger Braid.

Construction
Braided mainline; carrier count not established here
Listed strengths
10-65 lb
Line type
Braid main line

Grand Slam as the working braid

Use these exact Grand Slam rows when choosing braided mainline. The official 10 lb is .004 in/0.10 mm, much thinner than the catalog's unsupported old generic value. That small diameter can produce high capacity estimates; decide how much working line the fishing needs before treating a 300-yard package as mandatory reel fill.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Grand Slam Braid strength

Choose the actual verified 10/15/20/30/50/65 lb test for the rod, lure and cover. The old 40 lb row remains excluded until its exact product specifications are established.

Use the official GSB-F300 diameter instead of a generic Hi-Seas Braid entry. These mainline specifications do not apply to Downrigger Braid.

A large numerical capacity with fine braid is not a recommendation to fill every yard with braid. Select a useful working length and plan any remaining volume as backing.

This is a source-based specification and setup guide, not a hands-on review or comparative performance test.

Backing, working line & leaders

Match the GSB-F300 SKU

Confirm Grand Slam Braid in the Green 300-yard package. Downrigger Braid is a separate product and purpose.

Allocate the working section

Choose enough line for casts or fishing depth, likely runs and trimming. The remaining package can be retained instead of overfilling the reel.

Use proper braid attachment

Follow the reel maker's guidance for arbor grip, keep winding tension even and use backing only as the planned base below the working line.

Grand Slam Braid questions, answered

Is this Downrigger Braid?

No. The verified GSB-F300 SKUs are Grand Slam Braid mainline. Downrigger Braid is separately listed.

Where is the 40 lb row?

An exact current 40 lb numeric/package source was not verified, so the legacy row is excluded. No diameter is interpolated.

Why is the 10 lb diameter different from the old catalog?

The official current SKU title says .004 in/0.10 mm. The unverified older generic diameter is not used for this candidate.
