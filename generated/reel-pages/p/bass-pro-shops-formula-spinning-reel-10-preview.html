Best overall range: 6-10 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

Bass Pro Shops Formula Spinning Reel 10 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Bass Pro Shops Formula Spinning Reel 10?

6-10 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Bass Pro Shops Formula Spinning Reel 10 Hold?

The reel is published at 4 lb / 200 yards, 6 lb / 140 yards, 8 lb / 110 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 200 yards, 20 lb / 140 yards, 30 lb / 110 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
