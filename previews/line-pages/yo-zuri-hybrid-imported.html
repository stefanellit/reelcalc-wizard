Yo-Zuri Hybrid

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Yo-Zuri Hybrid is a nylon/fluorocarbon copolymer, not 100% fluorocarbon. This guide uses Clear packages with verified yard lengths: 275 yards in 4-20 lb and 600 yards in 6-40 lb. Mass-labeled heavy bulk spools are not assigned invented yardages.

Construction
Copolymer: nylon and fluorocarbon molecularly bonded during extrusion
Listed strengths
4-40 lb
Line type
Copolymer main line

Is Hybrid right for your setup?

Hybrid is a candidate for a full copolymer mainline when you want the nylon/fluorocarbon blend rather than a braid or a pure fluorocarbon. Its diameter is not interchangeable with TopKnot or T7 at the same pound test. Decide whether one reel or several fills justify the larger package, and keep thick sizes off compact spinning spools unless the reel and presentation suit them.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Hybrid strength

The Clear 4 lb size is verified in 275 yards, while 6-20 lb have both 275- and 600-yard listings. Do not apply the 600-yard option to 4 lb without an actual source row.

Hybrid 15 lb is listed at 0.016 inch and 0.405 mm; 20 lb is 0.017 inch and 0.438 mm. Treat the increase in physical size separately from the pound-test label.

The verified 25, 30 and 40 lb Clear rows use 600-yard packages. Higher strengths appear in a High Visibility mass-spool table, but its three-pound package weight does not establish line length.

Specification-based guidance, not a ReelCalc hands-on performance test. Capacity results are estimates, not measured fills.

Backing, working line & leaders

Choose a Clear filler by strength

Check 275 versus 600 yards for the selected strength. A long retail spool may supply several working fills, but divide by calculated yardage rather than assuming a fixed number of reels.

Do not convert spool weight into length

A one- or three-pound bulk package is a mass-labeled offering. Without manufacturer yardage it cannot be used as a yard-length purchase option in this calculator.

Keep Hybrid and Hybrid Ice separate

The dedicated Ice version uses 55-yard packages and different light-strength diameters. Selecting the same pound label does not make the two product charts interchangeable.

Hybrid questions, answered

Is Hybrid 100% fluorocarbon?

No. Yo-Zuri describes a molecularly bonded nylon/fluorocarbon copolymer. That classification is preserved here.

Why are the heaviest strengths excluded?

The official High Visibility chart lists 50-130 lb in three-pound bulk spools without yard lengths. Their diameters are preserved as evidence, but no usable retail yardage has been verified.

Does 4 lb Hybrid have the same diameter as Hybrid Ice?

No. Clear Hybrid 4 lb is listed at 0.235 mm; Hybrid Ice 4 lb is listed at 0.203 mm. Use the actual product.

How much Hybrid will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 275-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
