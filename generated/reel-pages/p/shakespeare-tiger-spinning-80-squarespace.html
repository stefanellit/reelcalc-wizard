Shakespeare Tiger Spinning 80 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Tiger 80 is the largest of these five spinning models, with a 25 lb / 290-yard mono reference and 4.4:1 gearing. The thicker line used for heavier bait fishing takes up more space, so check your chosen line against the diameter ratings below. Stren clear monofilament comes pre-spooled on the reel. Use this reel's calculator to plan your refill with or without backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shakespeare Tiger Spinning 80

Best overall range: 30-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 20-30 lb mono

For a straightforward refill, start with 20-30 lb mono/fluoro and match it to your rod. The braid range is a ReelCalc suggestion, not a published braid rating from Shakespeare.

Best Line Setup for the Shakespeare Tiger Spinning 80

The reel comes with Stren clear mono. When replacing it, 20-30 lb mono/fluoro is a starting range to consider alongside your rod rating. You can fill with main line or use optional backing. For braid, secure the base against slipping and watch the actual fill level because Shakespeare does not publish a braid capacity for this model.

Shakespeare Tiger Spinning 80 FAQs
