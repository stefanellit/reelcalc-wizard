Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Mastiff FC is Shimano's NANOARMOR fluorocarbon main line. This guide covers the 90-meter version listed by Shimano Brazil, not the US 200-yard packages. Compare its diameter with your reel capacity and plan a working length with enough reserve before adding backing.

Is Mastiff FC right for your setup?

Mastiff FC is a fluorocarbon main-line option with Shimano's NANOARMOR surface structure. The 90-meter package covered here needs a deliberate working-length plan: check that it leaves enough line for your casts, fish runs and retying. Use backing beneath that working section if the reel holds more, or choose a longer supply when the fishing requires it.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Mastiff FC strength

The exact 5 lb diameter is .185 mm, and 6 lb is .205 mm. Those product-specific numbers should not be replaced with retailer values rounded to two decimal places.

The 10 lb entry is .260 mm and 16 lb is .330 mm. Choose the appropriate tackle first, then recalculate capacity as diameter increases.

The 6 lb strength is verified under numeric product code/japanAdminCode 868572 even though it was absent from the starting catalog inventory. It gets its own strength ID, not the existing 5 lb ID.

Backing, working line & leaders

90 meters is not 200 yards

A 90-meter package contains about 98.43 yards. This page does not present a US 200-yard package as an interchangeable size for the same regional product code.

Keep knots below the working section

With less than 100 yards in the retail package, assess casting distance, runs, and future trimming before winding the entire package over backing.

Check FC, 90m and product code

The product is fluorocarbon despite the site's broad URL category. Match the package identity and order code, not a category name or similar family label.

Mastiff FC questions, answered

Does this verify US Mastiff filler spools?

No. The verified source here is the Shimano pt-BR MASTIFF FC 90m table. US 200-yard stock requires its own exact product code evidence.

Are these inch values official?

The manufacturer table supplies millimeters. Inch values are calculated conversions, clearly marked as such.

Does NANOARMOR make any knot reliable?

No. It is Shimano's surface-structure technology, not a replacement for a suitable knot, careful tying, inspection, and an appropriate drag setting.
