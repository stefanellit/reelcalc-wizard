Reviewed 2026-09-13 independently of the research agent. Scope: all 15 proposed rows across Trilene XT, Trilene 100% Fluorocarbon, and Forward Finesse Braid, plus gold-standard copy and edition restrictions. No publication approval is implied by this source review.

- Trilene XT: visually checked the archived manufacturer chart and its cover. The cover explicitly says 2020 Product Guide, despite the archive's 2021 directory name. All ten Clear filler-spool rows are historical 2020 US specifications. The 14 lb row must not be mislabeled 15 lb. The page heading, introduction, source note and chart caption identify this historical scope.
- Trilene 100% Fluorocarbon: visually checked three manufacturer package photographs. Retained 10 lb at .012 in / .30 mm on 2,000 yd bulk; 15 lb at .015 in / .38 mm on 200 yd filler; 20 lb at .017 in / .43 mm on 200 yd filler. No other strength/package pair is inferred from a blank or retailer-authored chart.
- Forward Finesse Braid: visually checked manufacturer package labels for 10 lb at .007 in / .17 mm and 12 lb at .008 in / .20 mm, both 150 yd / 137 m. The printed 4 lb and 6 lb mono equivalents are not actual braid strengths. No 150 m regional package is substituted.
