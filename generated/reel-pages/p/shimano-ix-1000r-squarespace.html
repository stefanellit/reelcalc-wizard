Shimano IX 1000R Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Shimano IX 1000R (IX1000R) is a 1000-size rear-drag spinning reel. It weighs 8.4 oz, brings in 19 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 7 lb. For small lures, start with line that suits your rod and check its diameter as well as its pound test. Explore line options below, then use this reel's preloaded calculator for either a full spool of main line or a setup with backing underneath.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano IX 1000R

Best overall range: 6-10 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 6-10 lb braid and add a 2-6 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 2-6 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Shimano IX 1000R For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing panfish, trout, and finesse freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 2-6 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 6-10 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Shimano IX 1000R

A practical starting point for the Shimano IX 1000R is 6 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 2-6 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Shimano IX 1000R Specs

The Shimano IX 1000R uses a 4.1:1 gear ratio and retrieves 19 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 8.4 oz and has a published maximum drag of 7 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Shimano IX 1000R FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano IX 1000R?

6-10 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Shimano IX 1000R Hold?

The reel is published at 2 lb / 270 yards, 4 lb / 140 yards, 6 lb / 110 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 10 lb / 95 yards, 15 lb / 85 yards, 20 lb / 65 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
