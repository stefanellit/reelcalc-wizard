Shimano Nasci FB 2500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

For freshwater bass and walleye fishing, the Shimano Nasci FB 2500 pairs 5:1 gearing with a listed 29-inch retrieve per turn at full spool. In the 1000-C5000 models, HAGANE Gear and X-Ship support the drivetrain when retrieving lures or winding against resistance. Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano Nasci FB 2500

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 6-12 lb mono/fluoro

10-20 lb braid is a ReelCalc starting suggestion for this setup. For a straight main line, consider 6-12 lb mono/fluoro. Match the strength to your rod, presentation and cover; backing is optional.

Shimano Nasci FB 2500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Nasci FB 2500?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Choose within that range to suit your rod, lure or bait, and the cover you fish. The lighter end helps with smaller presentations and line capacity; the stronger end offers more margin when the rig or cover calls for it. Choose a leader to suit the fish and conditions.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Nasci FB 2500 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 8 lb / 140 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
