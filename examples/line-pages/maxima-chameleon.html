Maxima Chameleon is a monofilament distinguished by the manufacturer's color-adapting formulation. The U.S. package-specific evidence covers mainline spools, while short Leader Wheels and three bulk variants missing numeric fields are excluded.

Is Chameleon right for your setup?

Chameleon deserves comparison when Maxima's color-changing positioning is relevant to your water. That claim does not establish invisibility in every condition or superior strength to Ultragreen. The useful setup decision is still the offered strength and physical diameter: a 10 lb Chameleon fill uses the published 0.012-inch figure, not a generic 10 lb mono estimate.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Chameleon strength

The 4-8 lb options run from 0.007 to 0.010 inch. Consider these with a rod and drag suitable for light line; abrasion-resistant marketing does not remove the need to inspect worn line.

At 10-18 lb the source offers 10, 12, 15 and 18 lb steps. The 10 lb and 12 lb rows have supported 220-yard One Shot packages, even though some Service variants lack numeric fields.

Chameleon 20-60 lb is progressively thicker mono. Use the heavy rows for an appropriately sized reel and application; extra nominal strength alone does not make a compact spinning setup more usable.

This is a specification-based Chameleon setup guide, not a ReelCalc hands-on casting, abrasion or breaking-strength test.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Choose a supported package

One Shot and other complete variants are linked to their printed yard fields. Three current Service variants at 8, 10 and 12 lb lack those fields and are deliberately not assigned guessed lengths.

Do not confuse a leader wheel with a reel fill

Chameleon is also sold in small leader-oriented packages. Those are not included in the mainline package map, and a short leader on braid is not a Chameleon full-fill setup.

Keep a useful working section

Use enough fresh Chameleon for the longest cast, runs and replacements. Optional backing belongs underneath that section, with the connection out of normal use and without forcing excess line above the recommended fill level.

Chameleon questions, answered

What does Chameleon mean?

Maxima describes a formulation that changes color hues with surrounding water and light. This guide reports that positioning without claiming a measured underwater visibility advantage.

Is Chameleon simply Ultragreen in another color?

They are separately named Maxima products. Similar listed diameters do not establish identical formulation or handling; use the specifications for the product actually on the spool.

Why are some Chameleon bulk packages missing?

The current official 8, 10 and 12 lb Service variants include no numeric diameter/yard fields. Other fully specified packages at those strengths remain supported.

How much Chameleon fits my reel?

Use the selected Chameleon diameter with your reel's published capacity. Pound test is a product strength label, not a universal diameter. Stop filling at the reel manufacturer's recommended level.

Does Chameleon require backing?

Backing is optional for a full mainline fill. It can make sense under a shorter fresh working section, but the connection must stay below normal casting depth with enough reserve for runs and retying.
