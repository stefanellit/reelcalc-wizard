Okuma Helios SX 40 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Helios SX 40?

15-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Choose within that range to suit your rod, lure or bait, and the cover you fish. The lighter end helps with smaller presentations and line capacity; the stronger end offers more margin when the rig or cover calls for it. Choose a leader to suit the fish and conditions.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Helios SX 40 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 10 lb / 220 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
