PENN Wrath II 5000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The PENN Wrath II 5000 (WRTHII5000C) is a 5000-size spinning reel. It weighs 21 oz, brings in 36 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 20 lb. For an inshore setup, choose your main line and leader for the fish and structure you expect, then check how much main line will fit.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Wrath II 5000

Best overall range: 20-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-15 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-40 lb braid and add a 12-15 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 12-15 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Wrath II 5000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, surf, pier, and live bait. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 12-15 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 20-40 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the PENN Wrath II 5000

A practical starting point for the PENN Wrath II 5000 is 20 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 12-15 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

PENN Wrath II 5000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Wrath II 5000?

20-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the PENN Wrath II 5000 Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 225 yards, 15 lb / 200 yards, 20 lb / 135 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 420 yards, 30 lb / 300 yards, 40 lb / 240 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
