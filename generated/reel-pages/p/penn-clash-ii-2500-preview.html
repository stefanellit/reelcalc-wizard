The PENN Clash II 2500 (CLAII2500) is a 2500-size spinning reel. It weighs 8.8 oz, brings in 33 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 12 lb. Match your main line and leader to the fish and structure you expect to encounter. Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Clash II 2500

Best overall range: 10-15 lb braid

Simple alternative: 6-8 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-15 lb braid and add a 6-8 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 6-8 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Clash II 2500 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing finesse bass, walleye, trout, and light inshore. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 6-8 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-15 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the PENN Clash II 2500

A practical starting point for the PENN Clash II 2500 is 10 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 6-8 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

PENN Clash II 2500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Clash II 2500?

10-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the PENN Clash II 2500 Hold?

The reel is published at 6 lb / 255 yards, 8 lb / 175 yards, 10 lb / 140 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 10 lb / 240 yards, 15 lb / 220 yards, 20 lb / 160 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
