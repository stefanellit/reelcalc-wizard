Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D (EXELT2000D) is a 2000-size spinning reel suited to trout, panfish, and finesse bass. 6.3 oz, 29.6 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 11 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. A published maximum drag of 11 lb adds useful headroom for bass and walleye while the reel remains sized for lighter tackle.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D

Best overall range: 8-15 lb braid

Simple alternative: 4-8 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 8-15 lb braid and add a 4-8 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 4-8 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing trout, panfish, and finesse bass. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 4-8 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 8-15 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

A practical starting point for the Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D is 8 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 4-8 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D?

8-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Exceler LT 2000D Hold?

The reel is published at 4 lb / 340 yards, 6 lb / 220 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 8 lb / 280 yards, 10 lb / 215 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
