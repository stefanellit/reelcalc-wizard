Quick Answer: Best Line for the Lew's MACH Bottomland Spinning 100

Best overall range: 8-10 lb braid

Simple alternative: 4-6 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 8-10 lb braid and add a 4-6 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 4-6 lb mono/fluoro.

Who Is the Lew's MACH Bottomland Spinning 100 For?

For light freshwater fishing, start with the rod's line range and the cover you expect to fish. Lew's MACH Bottomland Spinning 100 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's MACH Bottomland Spinning 100?

Start with 8-10 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. For a braid-and-leader setup, choose the leader for the cover and presentation rather than matching the braid label alone. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the Lew's MACH Bottomland Spinning 100 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
