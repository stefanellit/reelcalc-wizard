Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

This guide covers Stren FluoroCast Clear from the 2014 US catalog: 4-17 lb on 100- and 200-yard spools. It is listed separately from Stren 100% Fluoro, which has its own diameters. These are older specifications, so check the label and condition of the line you own.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Fluorocast strength

The 4-8 lb entries are the light end of this edition. Use them only where tackle and conditions suit the strength; extra capacity cannot compensate for an undersized line.

The 10 and 12 lb entries are .012 and .013 inch respectively in the FluoroCast table. Do not borrow the neighboring 100% Fluoro chart, whose strength-to-diameter mapping differs.

The upper verified steps are 15 and 17 lb, at .015 and .016 inch. A 20 lb FluoroCast row is not in this source and remains excluded.

No independent casting, knot, shock or underwater visibility test was performed. Manufacturer handling and visibility statements are positioning, not a ReelCalc guarantee.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Read FluoroCast on the package

Look for the gray-and-black FluoroCast family, not the blue 100% Fluoro package. Both contain fluorocarbon, but shared material is not proof of the same diameter or specification.

Compare 100 and 200 yards

The 2014 chart assigns both pony and filler formats to every listed 4-17 lb strength. Use the 200-yard option for a larger fresh fill or check a 100-yard working section against the required reserve.

Keep backing below the working line

If you retain backing, leave the connection below the longest expected cast and extra line needed during a fight. Use the calculator's exact diameter input for both sections.

Inspect before loading

Check the package identity, line condition and a carefully seated knot. Wind evenly and follow the reel's specified fill clearance; historical packaging does not establish the condition of an old spool.

Fluorocast questions, answered

Is FluoroCast the same as Stren 100% Fluoro?

No equivalence is established. The 2014 catalog presents them as separate named families with separate diameter tables and colors. This guide uses FluoroCast Clear only.

Why is 20 lb excluded?

FluoroCast's 2014 table ends at 17 lb. The 20 lb row belongs to the neighboring 100% Fluoro family and cannot be transferred.

Are 100-yard packages leader spools?

The source calls them pony spools for the FluoroCast mainline family. A shorter package can support a working section, but this chart does not recategorize the product as leader-only.

Do both lengths apply to each listed strength?

Yes. The 2014 Clear table explicitly covers 4-17 lb for both 100-yard pony and 200-yard filler formats. That is a historical specification, not a current stock statement.

Are the millimeter values also printed?

No. The chart prints inches. Millimeters here are calculated as inches multiplied by 25.4, without importing a different regional table.
