PENN Fierce V 8000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

If your surf setup calls for heavier line and plenty in reserve, the Fierce V 8000 has a published 25 lb / 310-yard mono rating. Its 5.3:1 gearing is slower than the 5000 and 6000, while the larger spool still retrieves a listed 44 inches per turn at the rated fill level. PENN fits HT-100 carbon fiber drag washers. The calculator below is set to this exact reel, with both full-spool and optional-backing setups available.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Fierce V 8000

Best overall range: 40-65 lb braid

Simple alternative: 20-30 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 40-65 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 20-30 lb mono.

PENN Fierce V 8000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Fierce V 8000?

Start with 40-65 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. For a braid-and-leader setup, choose the leader for the cover and presentation rather than matching the braid label alone. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the PENN Fierce V 8000 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
