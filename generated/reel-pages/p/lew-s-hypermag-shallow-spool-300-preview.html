Quick Answer: Best Line for the Lew's HyperMag Shallow Spool 300

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 6-10 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-20 lb braid and add a 6-10 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 6-10 lb mono/fluoro.

Lew's HyperMag Shallow Spool 300 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's HyperMag Shallow Spool 300?

Start with 10-20 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. For a braid-and-leader setup, choose the leader for the cover and presentation rather than matching the braid label alone. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the Lew's HyperMag Shallow Spool 300 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
