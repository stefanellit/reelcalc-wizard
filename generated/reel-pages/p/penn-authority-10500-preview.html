The PENN Authority 10500 (ATH10500) is a 10500-size spinning reel. It weighs 37.3 oz, brings in 43 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 60 lb. Allow enough main line for the depth you're fishing and the runs you expect from the fish you're targeting. Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Authority 10500

Best overall range: 65-80 lb braid

Simple alternative: 30-50 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 65-80 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 30-50 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Authority 10500 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing heavy surf, offshore, and big saltwater. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 65-80 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 30-50 lb mono range.

PENN Authority 10500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Authority 10500?

65-80 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the PENN Authority 10500 Hold?

The reel is published at 30 lb / 435 yards, 40 lb / 360 yards, 50 lb / 255 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 845 yards, 65 lb / 780 yards, 80 lb / 540 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
