Quick Answer: Best Line for the Abu Garcia Beast Spinning 3000H

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-10 lb mono/fluoro

10-20 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting suggestion, not a manufacturer limit. A straight-line alternative is 8-10 lb mono/fluoro. Match either choice to your rod and fishing conditions; backing is optional.

Who Is the Abu Garcia Beast Spinning 3000H For?

Abu Garcia designed the Beast family around larger baits and greater cranking and drag demands. Choose this size to suit the rod, presentation and amount of reserve you need; a higher drag rating is not a reason to fish more drag than your line and knots can handle.

Abu Garcia Beast Spinning 3000H FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Abu Garcia Beast Spinning 3000H?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for lighter lures in open water. For the reel's inshore or mixed-cover uses, choose more strength when your rod, rig, and the structure call for it. A leader suited to the fish and structure matters too; the braid's pound-test label does not tell you the whole setup.

How Much Line Does the Abu Garcia Beast Spinning 3000H Hold?

The capacity table shows the manufacturer ratings for BST3000H. Your chosen line may be thicker or thinner than the line behind a pound-test rating. Use its diameter in the calculator and watch the actual fill level rather than trying to force on the last few yards.
