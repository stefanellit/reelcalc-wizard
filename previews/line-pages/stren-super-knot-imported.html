Stren Super Knot

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

This guide covers Stren Super Knot as documented in the 2014 US manufacturer catalog: eight 4-20 lb test steps, Clear and Lo-Vis Green, on 220-yard fillers. Super Knot is the named monofilament product, not later Power Knot or adjacent MagnaThin. These are historical specifications, not evidence of current stock or unchanged formulation.

Construction
Self-lubricating monofilament
Listed strengths
4-20 lb
Line type
Monofilament main line

Plan the fill around Super Knot's own diameter

The self-lubricating formula was Stren's knot-focused positioning, not a guarantee against knot failure. The practical starting point is the exact diameter and a suitable test rating for your tackle. Super Knot's .012-inch 8 lb row uses more reel space than a thinner 8 lb mono; a familiar strength label alone does not establish capacity.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Super Knot strength

The 4 and 6 lb rows are the lighter end of this edition. Choose strength for the tackle and conditions before considering how much extra line fits.

The 8-12 lb rows step from .012 to .014 inch. A 220-yard filler does not imply the same number of fills at each strength.

The upper steps are 14,17 and 20 lb. There is no 15 lb row in this exact chart; do not relabel 14 lb or import a later product's values.

No hands-on knot, casting or abrasion test was performed. Self-lubricating and knot-strength language is attributed manufacturer positioning, not a ReelCalc performance guarantee.

Backing, working line & leaders

Match the name and edition

Check Super Knot on the package and compare its diameter with this 2014 reference. A Power Knot package needs a separate source even when its test rating matches.

Budget the 220-yard filler

All eight listed strengths have the same published filler length in Clear and Lo-Vis Green. Compare that length with full-spool capacity and your planned working reserve before choosing a full fill or shorter fresh section.

Check knots under controlled load

Wet and seat an appropriate knot carefully, then test it before fishing. The catalog's self-lubricating claim does not remove the need to inspect damage or retie.

Stop at the reel's fill limit

Use even tension and the reel manufacturer's specified clearance. If backing is used for a short fresh section, keep the joining knot below the longest expected cast and reserve.

Super Knot questions, answered

Is Super Knot the same as Power Knot?

This source does not establish equivalence. The chart and image explicitly name Super Knot in 2014; Power Knot specifications are not transferred.

Why is 15 lb missing?

The 2014 Super Knot chart lists 14 lb and 17 lb around that part of the range. It does not list 15 lb, so that inventory row is excluded rather than relabeled.

Does self-lubricating mean a knot cannot fail?

No. That is manufacturer formulation language, not an independent guarantee. Knot choice, seating, wear and loading still matter.

Are both colors on 220-yard fillers?

Yes, the 2014 table marks Clear and Lo-Vis Green for all eight listed strengths under the 220-yard filler legend. Current stock is not established.

Can I apply this to a newer spool?

Only after checking that its exact product identity and diameter agree. A historical chart alone does not establish current formulation or package continuity.
