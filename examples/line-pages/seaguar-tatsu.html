Seaguar Tatsu Fluorocarbon

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Tatsu is Seaguar's double-structure fluorocarbon main line. Two fluorocarbon resins are combined in one line, with Seaguar emphasizing suppleness and strength. This guide covers the listed 4-25 lb sizes and helps you budget a full fill or a working section over backing.

Is Tatsu right for your setup?

Consider Tatsu when you want to cast fluorocarbon directly, especially when handling and the amount of premium line used matter to your setup. A partial fill over sound mono backing can reduce how much Tatsu is tied up below the working line. It does not remove the need to choose a manageable diameter: thick fluorocarbon can be awkward on a compact spinning spool even if the capacity number is acceptable.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Tatsu strength

Start with the lighter 4-8 lb options when considering a light spinning setup, then check your rod, cover, and drag setting. Tatsu's 4 lb and 6 lb entries are not interchangeable in a capacity calculation: their listed inch diameters are .006 and .008.

For baitcasting setups, compare the 10-15 lb choices with the demands of your lure and cover before moving into 17-25 lb. The heavier sizes are not simply better versions of the lighter line. Their extra spool volume can shorten the useful working length.

Tatsu 20 lb and 22 lb both round to .015 inch in the manufacturer chart, although their millimeter entries differ. ReelCalc's inch-based calculation will therefore give them the same capacity. That is a limit of the rounded inputs, not proof that the lines are physically identical.

This is a specification-based setup guide, not a hands-on Tatsu performance test.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Decide how much Tatsu you need

For a full Tatsu spool, use the full-capacity result. For a shorter working section, allow for casts, fish runs, damaged-line removal, and repeated retying before assigning the remaining space to backing.

Keep backing below the working line

Mono backing sits underneath the Tatsu. Choose a working length that keeps its connection below the line you normally fish, and do not reuse backing that is damaged or slipping on the arbor.

Match the retail package

The 200-yard package is listed throughout this range. The 1,000-yard package is not listed for every strength: the current chart excludes 4 lb and 25 lb. Check your selected row before assuming a bulk spool exists.

Tatsu questions, answered

Is Tatsu a main line or leader material?

Seaguar sells Tatsu as fluorocarbon main line. You can use a short piece as a leader, but that does not make Tatsu the material filling a braid spool. Calculate capacity using the actual main line.

Does Tatsu have the same diameter as InvizX?

Not at every strength. For example, the current 20 lb entries are .015 inch for Tatsu and .016 inch for InvizX. Use exact product-and-strength diameters instead of treating all Seaguar fluorocarbon as one line.

Will one 200-yard Tatsu spool fill my reel?

Select your reel and Tatsu strength to check. A result below 200 yards means one full retail spool should cover that calculated fill; a higher result calls for more main line or an intentional backing plan. Stop at the reel's recommended fill level.
