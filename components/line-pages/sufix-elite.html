Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Elite is Sufix's all-around monofilament with Micro Resin Technology. This guide includes the verified 4-20 lb U.S. rows. The heavier 25 and 30 lb package lengths conflict within the official source and are withheld.

Is Elite right for your setup?

Elite is a candidate for a straightforward mono mainline or for backing beneath another working line. Its 330-yard filler options can cover a full fill on many smaller reels, but the amount depends on actual diameter. The 12 lb diameter is 0.014 inch in the current chart; do not substitute a generic 12 lb mono diameter when checking a reel.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Elite strength

Compare 4-8 lb to the line range on a lighter rod, then check the reel. Lighter line on a larger-capacity spool may require more than you expect to reach the recommended fill level.

The chart increases from 10 lb at 0.012 inch to 12 lb at 0.014 inch. Treat that as a substantial capacity change, not just a two-pound step.

The verified 17 and 20 lb rows are 0.016 and 0.018 inch. On a small spinning spool, physical fit does not establish comfortable handling.

Elite questions, answered

Why are 25 and 30 lb missing?

The official description says those strengths are 250 yards only, while current variant length fields say 330 yards. They are withheld until the package identity is resolved.

What is Micro Resin Technology?

It is Sufix's named technology for Elite. Sufix attributes tensile and knot-strength benefits to it; this guide does not independently test those claims.

Does every Elite package use the same winding method?

The official description and feature list disagree on which lengths receive G2 Precision Winding. This page does not extend that package-specific claim to the entire range.

How much Elite will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 330-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
