Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Regal LT 6000D

Best overall range: 40-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 14-20 lb mono

40-50 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting suggestion, not a manufacturer limit. A straight-line alternative is 14-20 lb mono. Match either choice to your rod and fishing conditions; backing is optional.

Daiwa Regal LT 6000D FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Regal LT 6000D?

40-50 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For inshore and surf fishing, use the lighter end when casting distance and line capacity matter most. Move toward the stronger end around structure, heavier current, or larger baits, while staying within your rod's line rating. Match the leader to the structure and fish; heavier braid alone is not a substitute for a suitable leader.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Regal LT 6000D Hold?

The capacity table shows the manufacturer ratings for RGLT6000D-B. Your chosen line may be thicker or thinner than the line behind a pound-test rating. Use its diameter in the calculator and watch the actual fill level rather than trying to force on the last few yards.
