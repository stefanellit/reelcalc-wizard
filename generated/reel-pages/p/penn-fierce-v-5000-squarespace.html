Best overall range: 30-50 lb braid

Simple alternative: 15-25 lb mono/fluoro

PENN Fierce V 5000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Fierce V 5000?

Start with 30-50 lb braid, then match the strength to your rod and fishing conditions. For a braid-and-leader setup, choose the leader for the cover and presentation rather than matching the braid label alone. Backing is optional; check that braid is secured against slipping if you tie directly to the spool.

How Much Line Does the PENN Fierce V 5000 Hold?

Use the factory ratings in the capacity table as reference points, not a promise that every line of that pound test fits the same amount. Select your exact line in the calculator, and watch the reel's fill clearance as you spool under steady tension.
