Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

If you want more line reserve without adding listed weight over the original Tatula LT 2500D-XH, the 3000D-CXH is rated for 280 yards of 10 lb mono and also weighs 6.2 oz. Its ZAION frame and 36.8-inch retrieve suit freshwater spinning where slack pickup matters. This guide covers the original non-MQ TALT3000D-CXH, with a calculator for your chosen line and optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-10 lb mono/fluoro

10-20 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting suggestion, not a manufacturer limit. A straight-line alternative is 8-10 lb mono/fluoro. Match either choice to your rod and fishing conditions; backing is optional.

Who Is the Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH For?

For deeper-spool freshwater spinning and bass presentations requiring additional line reserve, choose your line around the rod, presentation and cover. This guide follows TALT3000D-CXH; do not substitute the capacity of a similarly named reel from another generation.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH

Start with the line range that suits your rod and fishing. You can fill with main line or use backing beneath a shorter working length. For braid, secure the base against slipping and spool under steady tension; stop at a sensible clearance below the lip.

Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for lighter lures in open water. For the reel's inshore or mixed-cover uses, choose more strength when your rod, rig, and the structure call for it. A leader suited to the fish and structure matters too; the braid's pound-test label does not tell you the whole setup.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Tatula LT 3000D-CXH Hold?

The capacity table shows the manufacturer ratings for TALT3000D-CXH. Your chosen line may be thicker or thinner than the line behind a pound-test rating. Use its diameter in the calculator and watch the actual fill level rather than trying to force on the last few yards.
