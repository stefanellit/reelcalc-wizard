Choosing your setup

Choosing your Vicious 100% Fluoro strength

Choose among the verified 6-20 lb standard FLO sizes, not a strength from another Vicious fluorocarbon family. The 6-10 lb group has 100-yard options as well as longer packages.

A 200-yard spool is the default supply choice here, but larger 500- and 800-yard variants may be useful for multiple fills. Supplied length does not determine the strength you should fish.

At 15-20 lb, check handling on the exact reel as well as volume. A thick fluorocarbon fitting on a small spinning spool is not proof that it will fish comfortably.

This is a source-based specification and setup guide, not a hands-on review or comparative performance test.

Keep a reserve above backing

Allow enough usable fluoro for casting, expected runs and retying, and keep the backing junction below that section. Stop at the reel maker's fill clearance.

Vicious 100% Fluoro questions, answered

Why is 6 lb .008 inch rather than .010?

The current official Vicious standard 100% Japanese Fluoro chart lists .008 inch. The older generic/retailer value is not accepted as current manufacturer evidence.

Is this Pro Elite?

No. Pro Elite has separate EFLO-family products and its own official chart. This page is standard FLO-family fluorocarbon.

Do all tests have 100-yard spools?

No. In this checked range, 6, 8 and 10 lb have current 100-yard variants. The higher checked tests start at 200 yards.
