Seaguar Red Label Fluorocarbon

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Red Label is Seaguar's value-oriented fluorocarbon main line, with manufacturer positioning aimed at baitcasting reels. The current range runs from 4 to 20 lb. Package length changes at the heavy end, so a Red Label spool is not automatically a 200-yard spool.

Construction
100% fluorocarbon
Listed strengths
4-20 lb
Line type
Fluorocarbon main line

Is Red Label right for your setup?

Consider Red Label for a fluorocarbon main-line setup when cost and regular respooling are part of the decision. Match its diameter to the baitcaster, rod, and presentation before deciding on a full fill. If you are looking for a light spinning line, do not assume every fluorocarbon marketed for baitcasting will handle the same way as a softer main line.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Red Label strength

Use the exact Red Label entry rather than borrowing an InvizX or Tatsu diameter. Red Label 12 lb is listed at .012 inch, compared with .011 inch for both of those other models; it occupies more spool space in the inch-based calculation.

The 10-15 lb options give several intermediate choices for baitcasting applications, but the correct choice still depends on lure, cover, and your rod. Moving to 17 or 20 lb reduces capacity and is not an automatic improvement for open-water fishing.

For a partial fill, leave enough working Red Label to absorb retying and damaged-line removal. The backing knot should remain below the line used during normal casts and fish runs, not arrive at the lure after a few trips.

This is a specification-based setup guide, not a hands-on Red Label performance test.

Backing, working line & leaders

Check yards, not just pound test

The assigned retail variant for 20 lb is 175 yards, while the lighter retail entries are 200 yards. Both the strength and the package length matter when checking whether one spool covers your plan.

Bulk line still needs a fill limit

A 1,000-yard package can cover multiple fills, but it does not change your reel's capacity. Use the calculation as a starting point and stop at the reel manufacturer's fill level.

Main line and leader are different roles

A short Red Label leader on braid does not use a full spool of Red Label. For that arrangement, select the braid as the main line in the Wizard and consider the leader separately.

Red Label questions, answered

Is Red Label a fluorocarbon-coated nylon line?

No. Seaguar identifies this product as 100% fluorocarbon main line, not fluorocarbon-coated nylon.

Why does 20 lb show 175 yards?

That is the assigned retail package on Seaguar's current product listing and chart. Unassigned 200-yard selector combinations are not treated as verified retail packages. A 1,000-yard option is also listed.

Why is the 6 lb diameter different from my other Seaguar line?

Seaguar's Red Label chart and selector list 6 lb at .007 inch and .185 mm. Equal pound-test labels do not require equal diameters across products. Check the package if your line is from a different generation or market.
