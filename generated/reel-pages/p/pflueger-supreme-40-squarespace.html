Pflueger Supreme 40 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

Built around a 40-size frame, the Pflueger Supreme 40 (SUPSP40X) is best matched to larger freshwater, light catfish, pike, and heavier spinning. 8.8 oz, 31.5 inches of line pickup per handle turn, and 15 lb of published maximum drag define the working scale of this exact model. The 31.5-inch pickup gathers line efficiently after a long cast, while the spool rating leaves useful reserve for sustained runs.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Pflueger Supreme 40

Best overall range: 15-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-12 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 15-20 lb braid and add a 10-12 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 10-12 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing larger freshwater, light catfish, pike, and heavier spinning. Best Line Setup for the Pflueger Supreme 40

A practical starting point for the Pflueger Supreme 40 is 15 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 10-12 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Pflueger Supreme 40 Specs

Pflueger Supreme 40 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Pflueger Supreme 40?

15-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Pflueger Supreme 40 Hold?

The reel is published at 8 lb / 285 yards, 10 lb / 230 yards, 12 lb / 195 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 10 lb / 320 yards, 14 lb / 280 yards, 20 lb / 200 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
