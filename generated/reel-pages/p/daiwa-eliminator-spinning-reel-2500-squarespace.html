Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500 (ELT2500) is a 2500-size spinning reel. It weighs 9.9 oz, brings in 33.2 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 13.2 lb. For a finesse setup, match your line to the rod, lighter lures, and cover you're fishing. Use the line suggestions and preloaded calculator below to plan a full spool of main line or a shorter length over optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500

Best overall range: 10-15 lb braid

Simple alternative: 6-8 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-15 lb braid and add a 6-8 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 6-8 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing bass, walleye, trout, and finesse spinning. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 6-8 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-15 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500?

10-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Eliminator Spinning Reel 2500 Hold?

The reel is published at 6 lb / 210 yards, 8 lb / 170 yards, 10 lb / 140 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 20 lb / 170 yards, 30 lb / 120 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
