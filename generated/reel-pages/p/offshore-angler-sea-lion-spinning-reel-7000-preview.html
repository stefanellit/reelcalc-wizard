Offshore Angler Sea Lion Spinning Reel 7000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Offshore Angler Sea Lion Spinning Reel 7000 (SLS70) is a 7000-size spinning reel. It weighs 25 oz, brings in 37 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 40 lb. For surf or pier fishing, allow enough line for your cast with some left for a fish's run. Fill the spool with main line, or use backing under the amount you want to fish. Offshore Angler Sea Lion Spinning Reel 7000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Offshore Angler Sea Lion Spinning Reel 7000?

50-65 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Offshore Angler Sea Lion Spinning Reel 7000 Hold?

The reel is published at 25 lb / 240 yards, 30 lb / 210 yards, 40 lb / 135 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 80 lb / 260 yards, 100 lb / 230 yards, 150 lb / 195 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
