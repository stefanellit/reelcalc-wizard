Daiwa Regal LT 5000D-C FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Regal LT 5000D-C?

30-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For inshore and surf fishing, use the lighter end when casting distance and line capacity matter most. Move toward the stronger end around structure, heavier current, or larger baits, while staying within your rod's line rating. Match the leader to the structure and fish; heavier braid alone is not a substitute for a suitable leader.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Regal LT 5000D-C Hold?

The capacity table shows the manufacturer ratings for RGLT5000D-C-B. Your chosen line may be thicker or thinner than the line behind a pound-test rating. Use its diameter in the calculator and watch the actual fill level rather than trying to force on the last few yards.
