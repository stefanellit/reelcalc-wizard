Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 (AH100G2) is a 100-size spinning reel. It weighs 6.6 oz, brings in 30 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 7 lb. For small lures, choose line that suits your rod, then check its diameter before deciding how much to spool.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100

Best overall range: 6-10 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 6-10 lb braid and add a 2-6 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 2-6 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing panfish, trout, creek fishing, and finesse freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 2-6 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 6-10 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100

A practical starting point for the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 is 6 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 2-6 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100?

6-10 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Lew's American Hero Spinning Reel 100 Hold?

The reel is published at 6 lb / 120 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 8 lb / 150 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
