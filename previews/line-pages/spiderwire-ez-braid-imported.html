Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Choosing your setup

Choosing your EZ Braid strength

The four original labels pair 10 lb with .18 mm, 15 lb with .20 mm, 20 lb with .23 mm and 30 lb with .30 mm. Use the diameter belonging to the package you choose.

The separate 3, 4, 6 and 8 lb Test Dia phrases are monofilament comparisons. They are not alternative braid strength ratings.

All four photographed packages say 110 yards / 100 m and Moss Green. No 300-yard or Hi Vis Yellow specifications are inferred from them.

The historical European catalog publishes a different kg-rated range with different SAPs. Matching a familiar pound value by conversion would not establish package identity.

ReelCalc transcribed original manufacturer labels; it did not measure diameter or test casting, abrasion resistance or breaking strength.

EZ Braid questions, answered

Which label number is the braid strength?

Use the large 10, 15, 20 or 30 lb rating. The smaller 3, 4, 6 or 8 lb Test Dia figure compares diameter with monofilament; it does not rename the braid.

Is the inch diameter printed on the package?

The reviewed EZ Braid labels print millimeters. The inch values in this guide are calculated from those millimeter values, not separately published measurements.

Can I use the European kg chart instead?

Not as a direct replacement. That historical range has its own SAPs, diameters, lengths and kg strength column, without a nominal-US-pound mapping.

Does this cover 300-yard or yellow packages?

No. The numeric source photos show only 110-yard Moss Green packages. Other packages need matching original evidence.

Are these current-production specifications?

The photographs are undated and their front labels do not expose individual SKUs. This reference does not establish manufacture date, storage condition or continuity with every later package.
