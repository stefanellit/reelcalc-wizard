Yo-Zuri TopKnot MainLine

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

TopKnot MainLine is the reel-filling fluorocarbon member of Yo-Zuri's TopKnot family. The current official page lists 6-25 lb and 200-/1,000-yard packages. TopKnot Leader and TopKnot Ice remain separate products.

Fluorocarbon mainline in Natural Clear

Line type
Fluorocarbon main line

Is TopKnot MainLine right for your setup?

Consider TopKnot MainLine for a fluorocarbon working fill when you want to compare its exact diameter against your reel. The word TopKnot appears on multiple products; the mainline role and package length matter. Do not calculate a full reel using the dedicated leader table just because the family name matches.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your TopKnot MainLine strength

The mainline 8 lb row is 0.235 mm, while the 10 lb row is 0.260 mm. Physical diameter, not the marketing family alone, sets nominal capacity.

The 12, 14 and 16 lb steps are 0.285, 0.310 and 0.330 mm. Use the exact offered strength; a guessed 15 lb mainline row is not in the source.

The 25 lb mainline is 0.435 mm. Check rod range and reel handling, particularly before putting a thick fluorocarbon on a small spinning spool.

Specification-based guidance, not a ReelCalc hands-on performance test. Capacity results are estimates, not measured fills.

Keep a useful working reserve

For a working fill over backing, keep the connection below normal casts and fish runs. One 200-yard spool is only enough if it covers that plan.

Check the exact diameter against T7

The reviewed T7 and TopKnot MainLine tables match at each listed strength. A different family name alone is not a capacity advantage; choose other attributes separately.

TopKnot MainLine questions, answered

Is this TopKnot Leader?

No. The official source calls this Mainline and lists 200 and 1,000 yards. The leader products have separate tables and packages.

Does TopKnot Ice use these sizes?

No. TopKnot Ice is separately listed in 1-6 lb and 55 yards. Its data is not merged into this page.

Are both package lengths supported for every included strength?

The official summary explicitly lists the 6-25 lb mainline range for both 200 and 1,000 yards, with one shared diameter table for those sizes.

How much TopKnot MainLine will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 200-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
