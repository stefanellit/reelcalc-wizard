Okuma Ceymar Tactical Green 3000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Ceymar Tactical Green 3000?

8-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when fishing finesse presentations in open water. The upper end can provide more margin around light cover or when targeting bass, but keep the line matched to the rod and lure so the setup still handles light presentations well.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Ceymar Tactical Green 3000 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 6 lb / 185 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
