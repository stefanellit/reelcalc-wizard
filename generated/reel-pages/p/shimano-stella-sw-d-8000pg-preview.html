Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

For a saltwater setup built around jigging or working heavier loads, the Stella SW D 8000PG offers the lower 4.8:1 gearing of these two 8000 models. Its listed retrieve is 36 inches per turn, compared with 42 inches on the HG. InfinityXross spreads load across the gear teeth. Shimano publishes PowerPro braid capacities for this model; use the calculator below to plan your chosen main line with or without backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG

Best overall range: 50-65 lb braid

Simple alternative: 16-20 lb mono

50-65 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting suggestion, not a manufacturer limit. A straight-line alternative is 16-20 lb mono. Match either choice to your rod and fishing conditions; backing is optional.

Who Is the Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG For?

This 8000-size Stella is for heavier saltwater work, not light freshwater finesse fishing. Plan enough working line for the depth or casting distance and the reserve your fishing calls for. Match the leader and drag setting to the rod, target fish and structure.

Best Line Setup for the Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG

Start with the line range that suits your rod and fishing. You can fill with main line or use backing beneath a shorter working length. For braid, secure the base against slipping and spool under steady tension; stop at a sensible clearance below the lip.

Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG?

50-65 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting range for this heavier saltwater reel. Choose within it for the rod, lure or jig weight, current and fish you expect, then select a leader suited to structure and abrasion. Shimano's published capacities use PowerPro braid; a different braid with the same pound-test label may fill differently.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Stella SW D 8000PG Hold?

Shimano lists PowerPro at 40 lb / 340 yards, 50 lb / 265 yards and 65 lb / 215 yards for this model. No mono capacity is published in the cited U.S. table. Select your exact line to estimate a refill, then check the fill clearance as you spool.
