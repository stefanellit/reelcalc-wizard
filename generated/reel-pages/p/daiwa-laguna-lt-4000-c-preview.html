Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C (LAGUNALT4000-C) is a 4000-size spinning reel. It weighs 10.2 oz, brings in 32.5 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 26.4 lb. For an inshore setup, choose your main line and leader for the fish and structure you expect, then check how much main line will fit.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C

Best overall range: 20-30 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-12 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-30 lb braid and add a 10-12 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 10-12 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing larger bass, pike, catfish, and light saltwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 10-12 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 20-30 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C

A practical starting point for the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C is 20 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 10-12 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C Specs

The Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C uses a 5.2:1 gear ratio and retrieves 32.5 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 10.2 oz and has a published maximum drag of 26.4 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C?

20-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Laguna LT 4000-C Hold?

The reel is published at 12 lb / 160 yards, 14 lb / 140 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 10 lb / 220 yards, 15 lb / 185 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
