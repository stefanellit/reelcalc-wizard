Daiwa Caldia LT 2025 LT4000-CXH FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Caldia LT 2025 LT4000-CXH?

15-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For surf fishing, use the lighter end when casting distance and line capacity matter most. Move toward the stronger end around structure, heavier current, or larger baits, while staying within your rod's line rating. Match the leader to the structure and fish; heavier braid alone is not a substitute for a suitable leader.
