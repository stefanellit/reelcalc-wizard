Quick Answer: Best Line for the Lew's MACH 1 Spinning Reel 300

Best overall range: 10-20 lb braid

Simple alternative: 6-10 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 10-20 lb braid and add a 6-10 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 6-10 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Lew's MACH 1 Spinning Reel 300 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing bass, walleye, finesse spinning, and all-around freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 6-10 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 10-20 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Lew's MACH 1 Spinning Reel 300 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's MACH 1 Spinning Reel 300?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Lew's MACH 1 Spinning Reel 300 Hold?

The reel is published at 10 lb / 145 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 250 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
