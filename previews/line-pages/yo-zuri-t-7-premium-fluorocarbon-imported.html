Yo-Zuri T-7 Premium Fluorocarbon

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

T7 Premium is Yo-Zuri's bass-focused fluorocarbon mainline in 6-25 lb, with 200- and 1,000-yard packages. Yo-Zuri describes a heating process and a balance of tensile, shock and abrasion properties; these are manufacturer claims, not ReelCalc test results.

Construction
Premium fluorocarbon mainline with a manufacturer-described heating process for suppleness and translucency
Listed strengths
6-25 lb
Line type
Fluorocarbon main line

Is T-7 Premium Fluorocarbon right for your setup?

T7 is a candidate when fluorocarbon will form the actual working fill on spinning or casting tackle. Its printed diameter table is the same as the reviewed TopKnot MainLine table at these strengths, but that does not prove the same formulation or handling. Compare package needs, rod suitability and the intended presentation rather than assigning a capacity benefit to the premium label.

Choosing your T-7 Premium Fluorocarbon strength

The 6 and 8 lb rows are 0.205 and 0.235 mm. Consider the lighter end for suitable lighter tackle, but let the rod and presentation set the strength rather than the word premium.

T7 offers 14 and 16 lb, not a 15 lb row in this source. Keep those identities intact instead of rounding the product name to a familiar size.

The 20 and 25 lb rows are 0.370 and 0.435 mm. Their greater spool volume and potential handling demands deserve a deliberate reel choice.

Backing, working line & leaders

Choose the 200-yard filler by capacity

A full fluorocarbon fill may use less or more than 200 yards depending on the reel. Check the diameter-based estimate before buying or adding a backing section.

Treat bulk as a multi-fill supply

The 1,000-yard variants explicitly match every listed strength. Plan total working line across reels, leaving room for trimming rather than promising an exact number of future fills.

Keep handling separate from capacity

Use the rod and reel's guidance for thicker fluorocarbon. The manufacturer's suppleness claims are not a guarantee that every size behaves well on a small spinning spool.

T-7 Premium Fluorocarbon questions, answered

Is T7 only for baitcasters?

Yo-Zuri explicitly positions it for both spinning and baitcasting. The chosen diameter still needs to suit the actual reel and rod.

Is there a 15 lb T7?

The reviewed official table uses 14 and 16 lb. No 15 lb row is included.

Does T7 hold more than TopKnot MainLine?

At the same listed strength, the reviewed tables use matching diameters, so they produce the same nominal capacity input. That does not establish equal real-world handling or formulation.

How much T-7 Premium Fluorocarbon will fit my reel?

Use the exact strength and the reel's published capacity. Pound test is not a fixed diameter, so equal-strength products can produce different fill estimates.

Is one 200-yard package enough?

Only when it covers the planned working length and an appropriate reserve. Backing can fill unused volume, but cannot supply more working line for long casts, deep drops or fish runs.
