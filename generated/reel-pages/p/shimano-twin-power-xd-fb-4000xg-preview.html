Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG (TPXD4000XGFB) is a 4000-size spinning reel. It weighs 8.6 oz, brings in 40 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 24 lb. For an inshore setup, choose your main line and leader for the fish and structure you expect, then check how much main line will fit.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG

Best overall range: 20-30 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-12 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 20-30 lb braid and add a 10-12 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 10-12 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing larger bass, pike, catfish, and light saltwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 10-12 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 20-30 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG

A practical starting point for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG is 20 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 10-12 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG?

20-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Shimano Twin Power XD FB 4000XG Hold?

The reel is published at 10 lb / 200 yards, 12 lb / 160 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 230 yards, 30 lb / 180 yards, 40 lb / 140 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
