Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 (CS200G2) is a 200-size spinning reel. It weighs 8.1 oz, brings in 31 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 16 lb. For a finesse setup, match your line to the rod, lighter lures, and cover you're fishing. Use the line suggestions and preloaded calculator below to plan a full spool of main line or a shorter length over optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200

Best overall range: 8-15 lb braid

Simple alternative: 4-8 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 8-15 lb braid and add a 4-8 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 4-8 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing trout, panfish, finesse bass, and light freshwater. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 4-8 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 8-15 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200

A practical starting point for the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 is 8 lb braid over monofilament backing with a 4-8 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200?

8-15 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the Lew's Custom Spinning Reel 200 Hold?

The reel is published at 8 lb / 160 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 200 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
