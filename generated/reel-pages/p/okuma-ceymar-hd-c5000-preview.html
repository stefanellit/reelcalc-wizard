Quick Answer: Best Line for the Okuma Ceymar HD C5000

Best overall range: 20-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 10-14 lb mono/fluoro

20-40 lb braid is a ReelCalc starting suggestion for this setup. For a straight main line, consider 10-14 lb mono/fluoro. Match the strength to your rod, presentation and cover; backing is optional.

Okuma Ceymar HD C5000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Ceymar HD C5000?

20-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. For heavier freshwater fishing, use the lighter end when you need casting distance and line in reserve. Move up for larger baits or stronger fish near cover, provided the rod and rig are suited to it. Choose the leader for the target fish, including bite protection where needed.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Ceymar HD C5000 Hold?

One published reference for this model is 12 lb / 245 yards mono. The full table above shows the other listed capacities. Select your exact line to estimate the fill, then watch the spool clearance instead of forcing on the last few yards.
