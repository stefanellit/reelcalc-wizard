Okuma Ceymar HD 3000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Okuma Ceymar HD 3000?

10-20 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Choose within that range to suit your rod, lure or bait, and the cover you fish. The lighter end helps with smaller presentations and line capacity; the stronger end offers more margin when the rig or cover calls for it. Choose a leader to suit the fish and conditions.

How Much Line Does the Okuma Ceymar HD 3000 Hold?
