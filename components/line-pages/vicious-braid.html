The standard green PE braid

Vicious Braid is a standard PE braid main-line option, with a leader selected separately for the fishing. Pay attention to the diameter step between the listed 30 and 40 lb sizes when choosing for a particular reel. Plan enough working braid for casting, runs and retying, then use backing beneath it if a full-braid fill would use more than you need.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Vicious Standard Braid strength

Select test for the rod, lure and cover, then use the Standard Braid diameter. At 20 lb the current chart is .007 inch, not the generic legacy figure.

The chart moves from .009 inch at 30 lb to .013 inch at 40 lb. Keep that published step rather than smoothing or interpolating it.

Use the larger tests only where your tackle and fishing justify them. Backing can occupy spare spool volume without choosing a thicker braid simply to fill the reel.

This is a source-based specification and setup guide, not a hands-on review or comparative performance test.

Vicious Standard Braid questions, answered

Does this cover Vicious No-Fade?

No. Standard Green and No-Fade are separately named products. These diameters come from the Standard Braid chart.

Why does capacity change sharply from 30 to 40 lb?

The official chart lists .009 inch and .013 inch respectively. This guide preserves that step instead of interpolating a missing diameter.

Are bulk lengths available at the checked tests?

The exact current variants for these 10-65 lb tests list 150, 300, 1,500 and 3,000 yards. That does not imply live inventory.
