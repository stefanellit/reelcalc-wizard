Berkley Trilene Big Game Monofilament

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Trilene Big Game is Berkley's heavy-duty nylon mono for anglers who want stretch, substantial line strength, and a large supply for respooling. Its diameter matters as much as its pound-test label: the selected size can be used as a working main line or as backing beneath another line.

Is Trilene Big Game right for your setup?

Big Game is worth considering for bait fishing, larger freshwater fish, and saltwater applications where nylon's stretch is useful. It also serves as economical backing. On smaller spinning reels, compare the actual diameter before choosing a heavy strength; a line can physically fit but still be awkward to cast.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Trilene Big Game strength

The 10 and 12 lb choices are the lighter entries in this chart. Check their diameters against the reel and lure you intend to use instead of assuming they handle like every other brand with the same strength label.

The 15 and 20 lb choices use more spool space and suit setups that can manage thicker nylon. A larger spool, heavier presentation, or bait-fishing application can make more sense than a small finesse spinning reel.

The 30-50 lb entries are substantial main lines. Choose them for a specific heavy-duty setup, not as a default upgrade for ordinary bass fishing. When Big Game is only backing, its diameter determines the length needed beneath your working line.

This is a specification-based setup guide, not a hands-on Trilene Big Game performance test.

Trilene Big Game questions, answered

Can Trilene Big Game be used for braid backing?

Yes. Use the Big Game strength and diameter as the backing inputs, with your braid selected as the working main line. This page's calculator instead treats the selected Big Game as the main line.

Will a 1,500-yard spool fit on my reel?

Usually it is a supply for several fills, not a single fill. The calculator estimates what your selected reel holds at the chosen diameter; stop at the reel's specified fill level.

Why is the 25 lb size absent from this chart?

The available manufacturer diameter listing conflicts with neighboring strengths. That entry is withheld rather than used for a capacity calculation. This does not mean the product is unavailable.
