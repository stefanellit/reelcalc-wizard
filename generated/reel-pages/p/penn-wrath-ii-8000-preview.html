The PENN Wrath II 8000 (WRTHII8000C) is an 8000-size spinning reel. It weighs 30.5 oz, brings in 44 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 25 lb. Allow enough main line for the depth you're fishing and the runs you expect from the fish you're targeting. Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Wrath II 8000

Best overall range: 50-65 lb braid

Simple alternative: 20-30 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 50-65 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 20-30 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Wrath II 8000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, offshore, large catfish, and heavy bait fishing. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 50-65 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 20-30 lb mono range.

PENN Wrath II 8000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Wrath II 8000?

50-65 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the PENN Wrath II 8000 Hold?

The reel is published at 20 lb / 340 yards, 25 lb / 310 yards, 30 lb / 230 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 475 yards, 65 lb / 390 yards, 80 lb / 345 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
