Who Is the PENN Battle IV 8000HS For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing surf, offshore, large catfish, and heavy bait fishing. PENN Battle IV 8000HS Specs

The PENN Battle IV 8000HS uses a 5.3:1 gear ratio and retrieves 44 inches per handle turn. PENN Battle IV 8000HS FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Battle IV 8000HS?

50-65 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the PENN Battle IV 8000HS Hold?

The reel is published at 20 lb / 370 yards, 25 lb / 335 yards, 30 lb / 250 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 50 lb / 515 yards, 65 lb / 420 yards, 80 lb / 375 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
