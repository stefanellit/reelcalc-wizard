Maxima Braid 8

Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

Maxima Braid 8 is an eight-yarn braided line sold in U.S. Ultragreen and high-visibility versions. The manufacturer describes a Triple Coating Treatment plus pre-stretching and heat fixing. This U.S. chart uses the exact 150- and 300-yard variant fields.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Braid 8 strength

The lightest verified U.S. Braid 8 is 10 lb at 0.006 inch. The next offered size is 20 lb at 0.009 inch, so a 15 lb generic braid selection must not be relabeled into this lineup.

The 30 and 40 lb diameters are 0.010 and 0.011 inch. Select for rod, lure and cover, then calculate space; the tight strength labels do not make these interchangeable with another eight-carrier braid.

The 50, 65 and 80 lb versions are 0.013, 0.016 and 0.018 inch. Keep the U.S. specifications separate from the newer regional Braid Ultragreen range rather than replacing the heavy rows with regional numbers.

This is a specification-based Braid 8 setup guide, not a ReelCalc hands-on casting, abrasion or breaking-strength test.

Backing, working line & leaders

Choose 150 or 300 yards by the working plan

Both lengths are matched to every verified U.S. strength. A 150-yard retail package is enough only if the planned working length and reserve fit within it.

Provide secure arbor attachment

A coating does not remove the need for a reel-approved braid attachment. Use a sound mono base when the reel's smooth arbor needs grip, and follow any braid-ready spool instructions.

Keep unnecessary volume in backing if useful

Backing can fill a deep spool below the amount of Braid 8 you intend to fish. Maintain enough braid above the connection for long casts, fish runs and trimming.

Braid 8 questions, answered

What does Braid 8 refer to?

The U.S. manufacturer describes an eight-yarn round construction. It also specifies Triple Coating Treatment, pre-stretching and heat fixing.

Is Ultragreen Braid 8 the same as Ultragreen mono?

No. The Ultragreen name also appears as a braid color. The mono and braid products have different construction and capacity figures.

Can a regional Maxima braid chart replace these rows?

Not without matching the exact product and market. This page is grounded in the U.S. Braid 8 store variants, including their own diameters and lengths.

How much Braid 8 fits my reel?

Use the selected Braid 8 diameter with your reel's published capacity. Pound test is a product strength label, not a universal diameter. Stop filling at the reel manufacturer's recommended level.

Does Braid 8 require backing?

Use the reel manufacturer's approved braid attachment method. On a smooth arbor, a sound mono base can provide grip; additional backing can reduce the braid needed for a planned working length.
