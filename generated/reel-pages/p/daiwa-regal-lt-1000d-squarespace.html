Daiwa Regal LT 1000D Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

For small freshwater lures, the original Regal LT 1000D gives you a 6 lb / 160-yard mono reference without moving up to a larger reel. Its Air Rotor is part of Daiwa's lightweight spinning design. This guide covers RGLT1000D, not the newer B-generation model. Choose your line below to plan a full spool or a shorter main line over optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Daiwa Regal LT 1000D

Best overall range: 6-10 lb braid

Simple alternative: 2-6 lb mono/fluoro

6-10 lb braid is ReelCalc's starting suggestion, not a manufacturer limit. A straight-line alternative is 2-6 lb mono/fluoro. Match either choice to your rod and fishing conditions; backing is optional.

Who Is the Daiwa Regal LT 1000D For?

For light freshwater spinning for small lures, panfish and trout, choose your line around the rod, presentation and cover. This guide follows RGLT1000D; do not substitute the capacity of a similarly named reel from another generation.

Best Line Setup for the Daiwa Regal LT 1000D

Start with the line range that suits your rod and fishing. You can fill with main line or use backing beneath a shorter working length. For braid, secure the base against slipping and spool under steady tension; stop at a sensible clearance below the lip.

Daiwa Regal LT 1000D FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Daiwa Regal LT 1000D?

6-10 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when fishing small lures for trout and panfish in open water. The upper end can provide more margin around light cover, but keep the line matched to the rod and lure so the setup still handles light presentations well.

How Much Line Does the Daiwa Regal LT 1000D Hold?

The capacity table shows the manufacturer ratings for RGLT1000D. Your chosen line may be thicker or thinner than the line behind a pound-test rating. Use its diameter in the calculator and watch the actual fill level rather than trying to force on the last few yards.
