PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

●New marking system with enhanced contrast
The bass line, named “BMS (Bite Marker System),” continues to evolve as a marking line.
Black markings applied between the visible pink and yellow-green colors create contrast, resulting in sharper visibility.
This new marking system ensures you never miss a single bite or change in the line, no matter the conditions.

 

●Reliable resistance to abrasion
Newly developed 100% fluorocarbon line with excellent abrasion resistance, allowing you to confidently target covers and structures.
Not only has the line's strength increased, but its flexibility has also improved, resulting in a more trouble-free feel during use.

 

・By sandwiching black between high-visibility colors, the “movement of line” becomes more clearly visible.
・Color formulation designed to resist fading even when used or left outdoors for extended periods in harsh environments like boat decks under scorching sun
・Markings at 1-meter intervals can also provide a rough indication of water depth.
・By re-examining the raw material, we have achieved a supple, trouble-free yarn quality that meets modern needs.
・The 320m roll is 80m × 4 times, offering even better value for money than the previous version.
・Includes lb stickers that can be applied to the reel, allowing you to instantly see what pound is spooled in the reel.

TECHNOLOGY

  • Parallel spooling

    A spooling system which minimizes curling and surface indentation. Feel the difference as the line comes off the spool nice and straight and very smooth.

  • Plasma Rise

    Through the joint development with Plasma Concept Tokyo, the world's first realization of continuous atmospheric pressure plasma treatment of fibrous materials, which has been regarded as difficult, has been realized.
    This makes it possible to apply plasma treatment to fishing lines, and to provide various performances. It is an innovative technology with unlimited possibilities that can meet the stoic requirements of anglers.

  • Remaining volume notice sign

    Product has the signs which notice us remaining volume when you winding line for reel.
    Signs(sticker or making) are difference by products.
