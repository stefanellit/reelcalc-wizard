Quantum Cabo 5000 Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The Quantum Cabo 5000 (CB5000.B2) is a 5000-size spinning reel. It weighs 16.6 oz, brings in 38 inches of line with each handle turn, and has a listed maximum drag of 35 lb. Allow enough main line for the depth you're fishing and the runs you expect from the fish you're targeting. Use the line suggestions and preloaded calculator below to plan a full spool of main line or a shorter length over optional backing.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the Quantum Cabo 5000

Best overall range: 30-40 lb braid

Simple alternative: 12-15 lb mono

For most anglers, start with 30-40 lb braid over monofilament backing. A straightforward all-mono alternative is 12-15 lb mono. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the Quantum Cabo 5000 For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing sealed saltwater, inshore, surf, and offshore. Its deep spool is designed around heavy line and substantial reserve, so 30-40 lb braid over monofilament backing is usually more practical than filling the entire spool with premium braid. Anglers who prefer a straight-line setup can use the published 12-15 lb mono range.

Quantum Cabo 5000 FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the Quantum Cabo 5000?

30-40 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end when line capacity and casting distance matter most. Move toward the stronger end for large baits, rough structure, heavy current, or situations where abrasion resistance and pulling power take priority.

How Much Line Does the Quantum Cabo 5000 Hold?

The reel is published at 15 lb / 255 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 30 lb / 400 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
