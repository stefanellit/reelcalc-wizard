PENN Battle IV 4000HS Line Capacity & Reel Setup Guide

The PENN Battle IV 4000HS (BTLIV4000HS) falls in the 4000-size class and is intended for inshore, larger bass, pike, and pier fishing. Hydro Armor seals critical areas to reduce water intrusion in harsh conditions, while CNC Gear uses precisely machined gearing for dependable power transfer. Its published 10 lb / 250 yard capacity baseline leaves room for working line and leader, while the 43-inch pickup supports quick line control around moving fish.

Quick Answer: Best Line for the PENN Battle IV 4000HS

Best overall range: 15-30 lb braid

Simple alternative: 8-12 lb mono/fluoro

For most anglers, start with 15-30 lb braid and add an 8-12 lb leader matched to the water and target species. A straightforward alternative is 8-12 lb mono/fluoro. Using more backing can reduce how much premium line you need.

Who Is the PENN Battle IV 4000HS For?

This reel makes the most sense for anglers fishing inshore, larger bass, pike, and pier fishing. Its published capacity gives you room to choose between a simple 8-12 lb mono/fluoro setup or a thinner 15-30 lb braid setup with a leader. That flexibility is useful when the same reel may see light presentations one day and stronger fish or mixed cover the next.

Best Line Setup for the PENN Battle IV 4000HS

A practical starting point for the PENN Battle IV 4000HS is 15 lb braid over monofilament backing with an 8-12 lb leader. Backing lets you put the useful amount of working line on top without filling the entire spool with premium line. Since two lines with the same pound-test label can have different diameters, use the listed diameter of the exact line whenever it is available.

PENN Battle IV 4000HS Specs

The PENN Battle IV 4000HS uses a 7:1 gear ratio and retrieves 43 inches per handle turn, which helps pick up slack without making the reel oversized for its intended uses. It weighs 12.8 oz and has a published maximum drag of 15 lb. Maximum drag is the reel's upper rating, not a suggestion to fish every setup at that setting.

PENN Battle IV 4000HS FAQs

What Size Braid Is Best for the PENN Battle IV 4000HS?

15-30 lb braid is the practical ReelCalc range for this reel. Use the lighter end for finesse presentations, trout, and open water. Move toward the stronger end for bass, current, vegetation, or situations where a little more abrasion resistance and control are useful.

How Much Line Does the PENN Battle IV 4000HS Hold?

The reel is published at 8 lb / 305 yards, 10 lb / 250 yards, 12 lb / 185 yards. Its listed braid ratings are 15 lb / 410 yards, 20 lb / 295 yards, 30 lb / 210 yards. Actual capacity can vary with the diameter of the exact line selected.
