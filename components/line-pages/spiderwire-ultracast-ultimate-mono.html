Diameter chart, reel capacity & backing guide

This guide covers SpiderWire Ultracast Ultimate Mono's 2018 US published Clear specifications only. It is the copolymer mono family, not Ultracast Braid or Fluoro-Braid. The chart retains that edition's 15 lb size and does not infer current stock, unchanged formulation or later package continuity.

A mono plan with an edition-specific diameter

Ultimate Mono was positioned around a thinner, lower-stretch mono concept. Treat that as manufacturer positioning, then make the practical choice from the rod, lure, cover and exact diameter. A full mono fill is a simpler starting point than a braid-style backing plan. Before using these historical inputs for a package in hand, check that its identity and labeling match the 2018 scope.

Choosing your setup

Choosing your Ultimate Mono strength

The 4-8 lb rows are the light end of the 2018 table. Choose them only where the tackle and conditions suit their test ratings; extra spool capacity is not a substitute for adequate strength.

The 10,12,15 and 17 lb entries use the same published filler length in this edition, but their different diameters still produce different reel capacities.

The 20 lb entry is both thicker and supplied in a shorter filler package. Plan its working reserve independently rather than assuming one package fills every reel.

Keep the later 14 lb entry out of this chart. A changed strength label and a later diameter typo cannot be resolved by joining rows from different editions.

No independent stretch, strength, abrasion or casting test was performed. Manufacturer performance positioning is not treated as a measured ReelCalc result.

At the spooling bench

Backing, working line & leaders

Identify Ultimate Mono first

SpiderWire reused the Ultracast family name across different line constructions. Match Ultimate Mono on the package. Neither the neighboring Fluoro-Braid page art nor an Ultracast x8 braid label supplies this mono's capacity input.

Choose the edition's filler length

The 2018 Clear filler chart uses 330 yards at 4-8 lb, 300 yards at 10-17 lb and 270 yards at 20 lb. These are the 2018 test steps, including 15 rather than 14 lb.

Start with a full mono fill

A separate anti-slip backing layer is not required for the reason it may be used beneath braid. If you choose a shorter fresh mono section over backing, leave sufficient reserve above the connection.

Confirm the final level

Wind with even tension and stop at the reel manufacturer's recommended clearance. Check a newer package's own label before substituting its line into this historical calculation.

Ultimate Mono questions, answered

Why is this guide specifically for 2018?

That official edition gives a coherent Clear diameter and package table. A later edition changes one test step and contains a conflicting 12 lb diameter, so the editions are deliberately kept separate.

Was the later .12-inch value corrected to .012 here?

No. The .012-inch figure is independently printed in the 2018 source used for this chart. The 2020 discrepancy is documented rather than silently repaired.

Are Ultimate Mono and Fluoro-Braid the same product?

No. They appear on the same historical catalog page but have different names and constructions. Only the Ultimate Mono section supplies this chart and image.

Why does the chart use 15 lb instead of 14 lb?

Fifteen pounds is the strength printed in the 2018 Ultimate Mono table. The later 14 lb entry is not merged into this edition-specific reference.

Does this establish current stock or formulation?

No. The page is a reference to 2018 US published specifications. A newer package requires its own label and formulation verification.
